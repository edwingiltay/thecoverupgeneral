Bal­kan In­sight on the 2.6 million euros the State spent, 21 January 2022

The passage

“The documents revealing the legal costs were published after a freedom of information request by journalist Edwin Giltay, who wrote a book about how the Dutch state tried to cover up information about its role in Srebrenica.” The tenth paragraph of the article, immediately below the quotations from Alma Mustafić and above the photograph of the Mothers of Srebrenica at the Supreme Court. The document is in English.

Why this document matters

Bal­kan In­sight reported on 21 January 2022 that the Dutch State spent over 2.6 million euros on legal fees in the Sre­bre­ni­ca cases between 2003 and 2021, and named Giltay’s freedom-of-information request as the source of those figures. The essay states that for the Ministry of De­fence, silence is becoming increasingly costly, and that this is literally true.

This is the report that brought those figures to an international readership. Bal­kan In­sight sets the money paid to the state attorney beside what the relatives receive, 15,000 euros for a widow and 10,000 euros for other family members. That compensation is paid only to the relatives of the group of about 350 murdered people from Srebrenica who were sent away from the Dutchbat compound on 13 July 1995. The report quotes the lawyer for the Moth­ers of Sre­bre­ni­ca saying the real cost is much higher. It is also one of the foreign publications that kept the affair in the public eye.
