Giltay to the Chief Pub­lic Pros­e­cu­tor in The Hague, 28 June 1999

The passage

“5. The com­plain­ant there­fore urgently but re­spect­ful­ly requests that you initiate an in­ves­ti­ga­tion into the actual course of events as set out by the com­plain­ant in an of­fi­cial police report still to be drawn up at the Haag­lan­den Re­gion­al Police Force in The Hague. The afore­men­tioned Mrs Bar­ba­ra Over­duin is de­mon­stra­bly guilty of what is laid down in articles 261 in con­junc­tion with 262 of the Criminal Code, whereby the com­plain­ant has been directly injured in his good name and honour, this being a criminal offence com­mit­ted by Mrs Bar­ba­ra Over­duin and ex­pressed as such in the enclosed me­mo­ran­dum/libel­lous writing, in which the com­plain­ant is, among other things, con­tra­ry to the truth, char­ac­ter­ised by Mrs Bar­ba­ra Over­duin to third parties as ‘com­plete­ly insane’, which for the com­plain­ant is an un­ac­cept­a­ble injury to his good name and honour.” Page 2, paragraph 5. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“5. Kla­ger ver­zoekt U dan ook drin­gend doch be­leefd om een on­der­zoek te en­ta­me­ren in­za­ke de fei­te­lij­ke gang van za­ken zo­als die door kla­ger ge­schetst wordt in een na­der op te ma­ken pro­ces-ver­baal bij het Re­gio­korps Haag­lan­den ge­ves­tigd te Den Haag. Voor­noem­de me­vrouw Bar­ba­ra Over­duin heeft zich aan­toon­baar schul­dig ge­maakt aan het­geen is be­paald in de ar­ti­ke­len 261 junc­to 262 van het Wet­boek van Straf­recht, waar­door kla­ger di­rect in zijn goe­de naam en eer is aan­ge­tast, het­geen een straf­baar feit is ge­pleegd door me­vrouw Bar­ba­ra Over­duin en als zo­da­nig ver­woord in de hier­bij mee­ge­zon­den no­ti­tie/smaad­schrift waar­bij kla­ger on­der­meer, in strijd met de waar­heid, door me­vrouw Bar­ba­ra Over­duin te­gen over­staan­de der­den wordt ge­ty­peerd als ‘vol­le­dig ge­stoord’, het­geen voor kla­ger een on­ac­cep­ta­be­le aan­tas­ting is van zijn goe­de naam en eer.” The complaint spells the name ‘Overduin’, as the Ministry’s own letters of 1999 do; elsewhere in the file it appears as ‘Overduyn’. In the original the words ‘volledig gestoord’ are in plain double quotation marks.

Why this document matters

With his letter of 2 March 1999 to the National Ombudsman, his response to Giltay’s complaint, the Minister of Defence enclosed the report of the MID’s internal investigation, remarking that its results spoke for themselves. In December 1999 the Om­buds­man reproduced the interview reports from it in his report 1999/507 as part of the Min­is­ter’s position. With this document Giltay had already put the characterisation ‘completely insane’ before the Public Prosecution Service in June 1999, asking the Chief Public Prosecutor in The Hague to investigate those words as slander and libel under articles 261 and 262 of the Criminal Code.
