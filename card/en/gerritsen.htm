En­dorse­ment by the lawyers for the Moth­ers of Sre­bre­ni­ca, 17 Feb­ru­ary 2015

The passage

“Edwin Giltay describes how his rights were sac­ri­ficed by the State to keep the lid on the cover-up sur­round­ing the Sre­bre­ni­ca roll of film. Once again, this feeds the sus­pi­cion that the State is re­spon­si­ble for the dis­ap­pear­ance of the no­to­ri­ous roll of film.” Page 1, the three lines of the quote itself, set in italics in the body of the message. Trans­lat­ed from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Edwin Giltay be­schrijft hoe zijn rechten door de Staat zijn op­ge­of­ferd om de doofpot rond het fo­to­rol­le­tje uit Sre­bre­ni­ca gesloten te houden. Andermaal wordt het ver­moe­den gevoed dat de Staat ver­ant­woor­de­lijk is voor het laten ver­dwij­nen van het beruchte fo­to­rol­le­tje.” The message itself sets the quote in single quotation marks and in italics, which appear here as ordinary quotation marks. The three lines run on as one paragraph. Directly above it the writer announces the quote, intended for the promotion of Edwin Giltay’s book, with the words ‘Zoals toegezegd’ (as promised).

Why this document matters

In July 2015 the Moth­ers of Sre­bre­ni­ca cited The Cover-up Gen­eral in their statement of appeal against the Dutch State. Their lawyers had followed the book from the outset. Five months earlier they had endorsed it in writing, in this message. In it Marco Ger­rit­sen sends the quote he had promised for the pro­mo­tion of the book, calls it ‘our quote’ and signs it together with Simon van der Sluijs, as lawyers for the Moth­ers of Sre­bre­ni­ca. The words that the Voices section carries under their names come from this email.
