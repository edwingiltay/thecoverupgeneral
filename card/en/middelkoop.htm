Willem Mid­del­koop on Twitter, 23 Oc­to­ber 2016

The passage

“Reality again turns out stranger than the wildest conspiracy theory. Proves that truly anything is possible. In the Netherlands too. Including threats etc ..” The whole of the post, the text set above the photograph. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Werkelijkheid blijkt weer meer bizar dan grootste complottheorie. Bewijst dat werkelijk alles kan. Ook in NL. Inclusief bedreigingen etc ..” Middelkoop writes in clipped Twitter style, without articles and with ‘NL’ for the Netherlands. His second sentence has no subject; the photograph of a page of the book that he posted with it shows that he means the book, and the quotation in the Voices section therefore supplies ‘The book’. The post closes with ‘etc’ and two full stops rather than three. It is a standalone post, not a reply.

Why this document matters

Middelkoop, an international bestselling author and journalist, photographed a single page of the book. It is the page where an experienced detective confirms, to his own dismay, that some of his colleagues intimidate citizens they regard as troublesome. He also confirms that such intimidation is carried out so that the victim can hardly prove it afterwards. At the foot of the page the Srebrenica veteran Derk Zwaan says the same was done to him. Above that photograph Middelkoop wrote that reality once again turns out to be stranger than the wildest conspiracy theory, and that this proves anything is possible, in the Netherlands too, threats included.
