Min­is­try of De­fence, Wob decision on the film roll, 2 August 2021

The passage

“After the return to the Netherlands, these images were unintentionally lost through human error during development in a Ministry of Defence photo laboratory.” Page 1, the closing sentence of the third paragraph. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Deze opnamen zijn na terugkomst in Nederland onbedoeld door een menselijke fout verloren gegaan bij het ontwikkelen in een fotolaboratorium van Defensie.” Line breaks in the scan have been closed up.

Why this document matters

In this decision of 2 August 2021, the Ministry of Defence writes that the photographs taken by a Dutchbat soldier near Potočari on 13 July 1995 were “lost” during development. On 13 July 2021 Giltay had asked for documents about an alleged order to an employee of the Royal Netherlands Army to destroy the film roll. The Ministry replies that no such documents were found and refuses the request. It refers to the NIOD report of 2002, which considered deliberate destruction unlikely, and to the parliamentary inquiry. The Ministry thereby repeats in 2021 its account that the images no longer exist. They have never been made public.
