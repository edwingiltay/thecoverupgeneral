Presentation of the second, revised edition to Phil­ip Dröge, 16 September 2016

The passage

“And yet, and I think this is the most impressive thing about you, you did not give up. You have persisted. And that is brilliant and means you are brave. Well done!” The complete text spoken by Philip Dröge in the clip, as given in the English subtitles that accompany it. The quotation in the Voices section condenses it to ‘And yet, the most impressive thing about you is that you did not give up. That makes you brave.’ The original Dutch wording is below.

Original wording

“En toch, en dat vind ik het allerknapste van jou, je hebt niet opgegeven. Je bent doorgegaan. En dat is heel erg knap en dat betekent dat jij dapper bent. Goed gedaan!” The spoken Dutch, as in the Dutch subtitle track of the clip on Vimeo.

Why this document matters

On 12 April 2016 the Court of Appeal of The Hague lifted both the full ban on The Cover-up General and the speaking ban on its author. Five months later the book was presented again in a revised edition, to which Giltay had added eight chapters on the battle against censorship. This short recording captures the moment the first copy of that edition changed hands, from the author to investigative journalist Philip Dröge, who called the author brave for refusing to give up. Nine months earlier, in December 2015, a court had ordered the author to stop distributing the book and to refrain from promoting it at lectures, book presentations and other public utterances.
