Ministry of the Interior to Giltay, 11 January 2001

The passage

“In answer to your letter of 14 October 2000, which Her Majesty the Queen has referred to me for handling, I inform you of the following. In that letter you complain about improper conduct by the Haaglanden regional police force while a criminal offence was being reported.” Page 1, the first two sentences of the opening paragraph of the letter. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“In antwoord op uw brief van 14 oktober 2000, welke mij door Hare Majesteit de Koningin ter behandeling is opgedragen, deel ik u het volgende mede. In deze brief klaagt u over het onbehoorlijk optreden van de regiopolitie Haaglanden tijdens het doen van een aangifte inzake een strafbaar feit.”

Why this document matters

In this letter of 11 January 2001 the Minister of the Interior replies to Giltay’s letter of 14 October 2000, which Her Majesty the Queen had referred to him for handling. The essay states that Queen Beatrix, at Giltay’s request, intervened when no action was taken on his criminal complaint, which was partly related to Srebrenica. On 14 December 2000, police force manager W.J. Deetman declared Giltay’s objection to the handling of his complaint about police conduct to be well founded on one point. That too comes up in this letter: the Ministry notes that Giltay had meanwhile received a letter from the manager of the Haaglanden police force in answer to his complaint, and refers him to that answer.
