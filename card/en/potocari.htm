The passage

“De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal · Edwin Giltay · book (printed book) | Dutch | 2016 · Groningen : Blauwe Tijger, 2016 · ISBN: 978-94-92161-21-5 · COBISS-ID: 52784646 · Sre­bre­ni­ca Gen­o­cide Me­mo­rial · not for loan: 1 cop.” Page 1, the record, field by field: title, author, format with language and year, place of publication and publisher, ISBN and COBISS-ID, followed by the holding library and its copy under the tab ‘Availability of material’. The fields appear separately on the page and are joined here. The page itself is in English, so this is the document’s own wording.

Why this document matters

The COBISS Plus catalogue lists the Dutch 2016 edition of De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal as held by the Srebrenica Genocide Memorial, where its copy is marked ‘not for loan’. The essay counts the memorial centre in Potočari among the collections that preserve the history the book describes.
