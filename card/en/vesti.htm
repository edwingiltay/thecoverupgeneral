Vesti, Serbian news site, on the ban, 24 December 2015

The passage

“Marijuana is permitted there, but a book about Srebrenica is not […] A court in The Hague has banned the author Edvin Giltaj from the distribution and sale of the book ‘De doofpotgeneraal’, as well as all promotional activities in connection with that work.” The headline and the standfirst above the article. Translated from the Serbian; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“Marihunana je dozvoljena, ali ne i knjiga o Srebrenici […] Sud u Hagu zabranio je autoru Edvinu Giltaju distribuciju i prodaju knjige “De doofpotgeneraal”, kao i sve promotivne aktivnosti u vezi sa tim delom.” The headline misspells ‘marihuana’ as ‘Marihunana’, and that spelling carries over into the page title and the web address. The standfirst gives the author’s name as ‘Edvinu Giltaju’, the Serbian dative of the transliteration Edvin Giltaj. The book title appears in straight double quotation marks in the source.

Why this document matters

On 24 December 2015 Vesti headlined its report on the ban ‘Marijuana is permitted there, but a book about Srebrenica is not’. The article notes that the book had been on sale for a year and that the Dutch media were shocked that it could be banned in a country that prides itself on its freedoms. The essay lists Vesti among the news outlets that reported the ban of 14 December 2015.
