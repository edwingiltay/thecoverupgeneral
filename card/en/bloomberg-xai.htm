Bloom­berg on xAI’s valuation of 200 billion dollars, 19 Sep­tem­ber 2025

The passage

“Elon Musk’s artificial intelligence startup xAI has raised new funding at a $200 billion valuation, according to a person familiar with the deal, making it one of the world’s most valuable startups.” The first sentence of the article, directly below the byline; this paragraph can be read without a subscription. The document is in English.

Why this document matters

Bloom­berg reported on 19 Sep­tem­ber 2025, citing a person familiar with the deal, that xAI, Elon Musk’s company, had raised over 10 billion dollars at a valuation of 200 billion dollars. Six weeks later, on 4 No­vem­ber 2025, Grok, xAI’s chatbot, endorsed The Cover-up General on X.

The essay cites this valuation alongside that endorsement: the post came from a chatbot, but it appeared on the official account of this company.
