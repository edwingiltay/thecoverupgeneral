Radio interview with Giltay before the appeal hearing, NOS Radio 1, 25 Feb­ru­ary 2016

The passage

“And that is, of course, an infringement of press freedom. […] I am going to win, I really believe that, yes.” From the recording, 1:35 to 1:45. Left out are Giltay’s sentence that this is why he appealed and the presenter’s question about how he rates his chances. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“En dat is natuurlijk toch een aantasting van de persvrijheid. […] Ik ga winnen, dat denk ik echt, ja.” The spoken Dutch, as heard in the recording.

Why this document matters

On 12 April 2016 the Court of Ap­peal of The Hague lifted both the full ban on The Cover-up Gen­eral and the speaking ban on its author. This recording was made minutes before that appeal was argued, and it catches the author on the morning itself. He explains that a former employee of the Military Intelligence Service, Barbara Overduyn, who appears in the book, had it banned, and that he will not cut the passages about her because the book is an autobiographical account, the form the court would later hold he was free to use. He calls the ban an infringement of press freedom and says he expects to win.
