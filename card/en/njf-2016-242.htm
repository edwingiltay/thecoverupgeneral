NJF 2016/242, the appeal judgment in the De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal case in a case-law journal

The passage

“Places of publication: NJF 2016/242; RAV 2016/75; IR 2016/92, UDH:IR/13439 with annotation by […]”
“Summary: Media law; Ban sought on reprinting the book De Doofpotgeneraal; Weighing of freedom of expression against the right to respect for private life.” The header of the judgment on jure.nl: the places of publication and the summary. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Vindplaatsen: NJF 2016/242; RAV 2016/75; IR 2016/92, UDH:IR/13439 met annotatie van […]”
“Inhoudsindicatie: Mediarecht; Verbod gevorderd op herdruk van het boek De Doofpotgeneraal; Afweging uitingsvrijheid tegen het recht op eerbiediging van de persoonlijke levenssfeer.” Dutch original as shown on jure.nl; the three places of publication are listed there one per line.

Why this document matters

This listing shows that the appeal judgment appeared in three legal journals: NJF 2016/242, RAV 2016/75 and IR 2016/92, the last with an annotation. The NJF text itself is only accessible to subscribers; the listing of these places of publication on jure.nl is public.
