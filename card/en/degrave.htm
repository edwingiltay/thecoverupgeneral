Frank de Grave and Jan Tromp, Grote jongen zijn, 13 Feb­ru­ary 2018

The passage

“I did not dismiss my most senior civil servant. I did not replace the top team. It is a terrible dilemma. Even in hindsight I do not know if I acted wisely.” Page 112. The sentence opens with ‘Dus toen heb ik het niet gedaan,’ (So then I didn’t do it); the quotation starts after those words. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Ik heb mijn hoogste ambtenaar niet ontslagen. Ik heb de top niet vervangen. Het is een verschrikkelijk dilemma. Ik weet ook achteraf niet of ik er verstandig aan heb gedaan.” Page 112, where the sentence begins ‘Dus toen heb ik het niet gedaan, ik heb mijn hoogste ambtenaar niet ontslagen’; the quotation starts at ‘ik’, capitalised here. The same words, in italics, head chapter 25 on page 109.

Why this document matters

Frank de Grave was Minister of Defence when the declaration that Giltay was insane went out in his name, signed on his behalf by his secretary-general, Dirk Barth. In this book De Grave returns to that period and sets out the dilemma of whether to dismiss his most senior civil servant. He did not do it, nor did he replace the department’s senior leadership, and he writes that even in retrospect he does not know whether he acted wisely.
