NBD Bib­lion review of the revised second edi­tion, 2016

The passage

“There has been quite a stir around the publication of this book. It was even banned for a time. For in ‘De doofpotgeneraal’, author Edwin Giltay tackles a painful subject.” Page 1, opening sentences of the review. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“Rond de publicatie van dit boek is nogal wat commotie geweest. Het is zelfs enige tijd verboden geweest. Auteur Edwin Giltay snijdt in ‘De doofpotgeneraal’ namelijk een pijnlijk onderwerp aan.” The review is set as one continuous paragraph; its line breaks are not reproduced in the wording above.

Why this document matters

This short review by NBD Bib­lion, the review service for Dutch li­brar­ies, opens by recording that the publication caused quite a stir and that the book had even been banned for a time. The reviewer sums the subject up as infighting within the military intelligence service and a Dutch three-star general who lets a spy of his own snoop around inside a private company, with the notorious Sre­bre­ni­ca film roll as the immediate trigger, and notes that the court cases are still ongoing. The author’s account of the affair, the review adds, reads like a thriller. The essay lists this review among the coverage of the revised second edition of Sep­tem­ber 2016.
