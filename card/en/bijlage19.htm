Di­rec­tor of the MIVD to Giltay about a CTIVD report, 24 Oc­to­ber 2017

The passage

“The CTIVD has not handled any complaint from you against the MIVD. There is therefore no CTIVD report either. In that connection I would refer you to the letters of 21 August 2009 from the Secretary-General and of 21 April 2015 from the Minister of Defence, in which you were informed that your complaint was not dealt with, in accordance with article 9:8 of the General Administrative Law Act.” Page 1, the second paragraph of the letter, which is its whole substance. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“De CTIVD heeft geen klacht van u jegens de MIVD behandeld. Er is om die reden ook geen rapport van de CTIVD. In dat kader wijs ik op de brieven van 21 augustus 2009 van de Secretaris Generaal en 21 april 2015 van de Minister van Defensie waarin aan u is medegedeeld dat uw klacht overeenkomstig artikel 9:8 van de Algemene wet bestuursrecht niet in behandeling is genomen.” The letter spells ‘Secretaris Generaal’ without a hyphen, and that spelling is kept here.

Why this document matters

In this letter of 24 October 2017 the Director of the MIVD writes that the CTIVD has handled no complaint from Giltay against the MIVD and that “there is therefore no CTIVD report either”. Giltay had asked the Minister of General Affairs for the CTIVD report on his complaint. Instead the service points to two earlier letters, of 2009 and 2015, which had told him his complaint had not been dealt with. The letter thus says the opposite of the ministerial letter of 6 June 2017, which had rejected his request for rehabilitation by citing an investigation by that same committee. The essay calls this the confirmation that the second pillar of that refusal, a CTIVD investigation, did not exist.

The letter is signed ‘for the Minister’ (voor deze), on behalf of the Minister of Defence, by the Director of the MIVD, Major General O. Eichelsheim, whose name and rank appear beneath it. Formally, then, it is the Minister’s letter, and with it the Ministry contradicts its own letter of 6 June 2017. The quo­ta­tion in the Voices section names the sig­na­to­ry, because it is the head of the service himself who here establishes that the CTIVD in­ves­ti­ga­tion invoked by his Minister does not exist.
