Index on Censorship, alert, 24 De­cem­ber 2015

The passage

“Netherlands: Court bans book on Srebrenica genocide […] 24 December, 2015 - A court in the Hague banned a book about the Srebrenica genocide and the role of the Dutch military that was published a year ago.” Page 1: the headline of the report and the first paragraph under ‘Description’. The report is marked ‘Verified’.

Why this document matters

Ten days after the judgment of 14 December 2015, the ban was listed on Mapping Media Freedom, the European platform that records press-freedom violations, as a verified report. The report is filed under ‘Legal measure’ and ‘Court/Judicial’. The platform was run by Index on Censorship together with the European Federation of Journalists and Reporters Without Borders, and the Dutch censorship case appears there alongside other violations recorded across Europe.
