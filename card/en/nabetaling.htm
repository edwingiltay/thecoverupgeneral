Sup­ple­men­ta­ry penalty payment by the National Ombuds­man, 26 June 2026

The passage

“26 June 2026 · Name: Nationale Ombudsman (NATIONALE OMBUDSMAN) · De­scrip­tion: re­cal­cu­la­tion of penalty payment further to case number SGR 23 / 6997 WOO · IBAN: NL58INGB0705003124 · Reference: re­cal­cu­la­tion of penalty payment · Value date: 26-06-2026 · Transfer · 100.00” The single transaction row, with the values from the columns Datum, Om­schrij­ving, Type and Bedrag (EUR). Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. The institution’s Dutch name, Nationale Ombudsman, is left as it stands, and the amount, printed with a decimal comma, is given here with a decimal point.

Original wording

“26 juni 2026 · Naam: Nationale Ombudsman (NATIONALE OMBUDSMAN) · Om­schrij­ving: her­be­re­ke­ning dwangsom n.a.v. zaaknummer SGR 23 / 6997 WOO · IBAN: NL58INGB0705003124 · Kenmerk: her­be­re­ke­ning dwangsom · Va­lu­ta­da­tum: 26-06-2026 · Over­schrij­ving · 100,00” The row runs across four columns and over several lines; the wording above joins them, with a middle dot at each field break.

Why this document matters

This bank statement shows a transfer of €100 from the National Ombuds­man, credited on 26 June 2026 and described as a re­cal­cu­la­tion of the penalty payment in case SGR 23 / 6997 WOO. The essay states that the National Ombuds­man mis­cal­cu­lat­ed the penalty payment and corrected the error only after Giltay had announced in June 2026 that he would take the matter to the civil court; the Ombudsman acknowledged the error on 11 June 2026, three days after that announcement. Here that ac­know­ledge­ment became money, 28 months after the initial payment.
