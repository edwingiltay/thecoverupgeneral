Ministry of De­fence declines to respond sub­stan­tive­ly to the book, 29 July 2020

The passage

“The Min­is­ter of De­fence has asked me to respond to your letter of 29 June. In that letter you state that you are working on a German edition of your book ‘De Doof­pot­ge­ne­raal’. You ask whether De­fence is willing to give a sub­stan­tive response to your pub­li­ca­tion. By this letter I inform you that De­fence has no need to respond sub­stan­tive­ly to your book.” Page 1, the body of the letter in full, the five lines between the salutation and the closing. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“De minister van Defensie heeft mij gevraagd te reageren op uw brief van 29 juni jl. In deze brief geeft u aan dat u werkt aan een Duitse uitgave van uw boek ‘De Doof­pot­ge­ne­raal’. U vraagt of Defensie een in­hou­de­lij­ke reactie wil geven op uw pu­bli­ca­tie. Met deze brief stel ik u ervan op de hoogte dat Defensie geen behoefte heeft om in­hou­de­lijk op uw boek te reageren.” The letter sets the book title as ‘De Doofpotgeneraal’, with both words capitalised, where the book itself is called De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal; it opens the title with a straight double mark and closes it with a curly one, rendered here as single marks inside the quotation.

Why this document matters

In this letter the Ministry of De­fence says that it has “no need” to respond sub­stan­tive­ly to the book, without giving any reason. The essay states that after the Su­preme Court judgment of 2019 Giltay again heard nothing from De­fence. In that case, in which the Neth­er­lands was held partially liable for around 350 Sre­bre­ni­ca deaths, the lawyers for the Mothers of Srebrenica had cited the book alongside dozens of other sources. In 2020 Giltay formally invited De­fence once more to answer on the merits, this time with a view to a translation. This letter is the answer.
