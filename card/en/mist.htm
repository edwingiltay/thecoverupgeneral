Giltay to the Minister of Defence, with the third, revised edition, 26 April 2022

The passage

“Your pre­de­ces­sors got it badly wrong at the time in the affair described in the enclosed book. I hope that, having read it, you will see your way to setting right the mistakes that were made.” Page 1, the body of the letter, which is two sentences long. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Uw voor­gan­gers zijn destijds de mist in gegaan met de affaire be­schre­ven in bijgaand boek. Naar ik hoop ziet u na lezing kans om de gemaakte fouten recht te zetten.” The Dutch idiom ‘de mist in gegaan’, literally to have gone into the mist, means to have got something badly wrong; the English above renders the sense rather than the image. The subject line sets the ordinal as a superscript: ‘3e herziene druk’.

Why this document matters

With this letter of 26 April 2022, Giltay delivered the third, re­vised edi­tion of the book in person for the Min­is­ter of De­fence, Kaj­sa Ol­lon­gren, with two sentences saying that her pre­de­ces­sors had got it badly wrong at the time in the affair the book describes, and that he hoped she would set right the mistakes made. The essay counts seven moments at three min­is­tries at which the State did not invoke a state secret, and concludes that this silence is not statutory secrecy but a choice. This letter follows those seven. No response came from De­fence, the essay states.
