Gen­eral Hans Couzy on the film roll, VPRO Radio 1, 10 July 1998

The passage

“[…] was like: well, then we have it at the military intelligence service, and then it is guaranteed a hundred per cent safe that it will go well.”
“That made it all the greater an embarrassment, according to Couzy, that things went wrong with the film roll.”
“That such a fuss was made about the film roll, I understand very well, because it is of course absurd that precisely that one photo on that one roll, and that that roll gets botched, and by the armed forces’ own military intelligence service at that. That is a story that naturally no one ever believes, that […]” The complete spoken text of the 30-second clip, transcribed from the recording. The clip begins and ends in mid-sentence. The first and last parts are spoken by Couzy; the middle part is the Argos voice-over. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“[…] was van: nou, dan hebben wij het bij de militaire inlichtingendienst en dan is gegarandeerd honderd procent safe dat het goed kan gaan.”
“Daarom was het een des te grotere blamage, aldus Couzy, dat het misging met het rolletje.”
“Dat er zo’n trammelant werd gemaakt over het fotorolletje, snap ik heel goed, want het is natuurlijk te gek voor woorden dat nou net die ene foto op dat ene rolletje, en dat dat rolletje verknoeid wordt, en dan door de eigen militaire inlichtingendienst. Dat is een verhaal wat natuurlijk nooit iemand gelooft, dat […]” The spoken Dutch, as heard in the recording.

Why this document matters

According to the essay, the official account of the failed development of the film remained contested, even within the armed forces themselves, despite multiple official investigations, and the former Dutch army commander gave his own account: the MID wanted to develop the film roll itself. In this clip he says he fully understands the commotion, because it is absurd that precisely that roll was botched, and by the armed forces’ own military intelligence service at that. He calls that “a story that naturally no one ever believes”.
