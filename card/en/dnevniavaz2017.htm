Dnevni Avaz interview with Giltay, 11 July 2017

The passage

“I want to express my deep respect for the victims. It must be stressed that Srebrenica still haunts the Netherlands because of everything that happened.” Page 1, the closing lines of the article, in the right-hand column beside the photograph, above the signature. Translated from the Bosnian; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Želim iskazati duboko poštovanje prema žrtvama. Potrebno je naglasiti da Holandiju još progoni Srebrenica zbog svega što se desilo.” The paper introduces Giltay’s words with a dash rather than quotation marks and closes them with ‘rekao je Giltej’ (said Giltay); neither is reproduced here. The wording follows the printed page; the words broken at line ends are joined up. The article spells the name Edvin Giltej, with the Dutch spelling Edwin Giltay in brackets, and gives the book title as Prikriveni general.

Why this document matters

In this interview in Dnevni Avaz of 11 July 2017, the day of the commemoration, twenty-two years after the genocide, Giltay says that his country’s Ministry of Defence still holds to the story that the film rolls were destroyed by accident, a story that, according to him, hardly anyone believes. He adds that he hopes parliament will decide, after the summer recess, to summon the Minister of Defence to settle the affair for good. He closes by expressing his respect for the victims and saying that Srebrenica still haunts the Netherlands, the words from which the headline is taken. The essay counts this interview among the coverage of the parliamentary pressure of 2017.
