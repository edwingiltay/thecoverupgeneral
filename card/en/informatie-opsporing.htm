Handbook In­for­ma­tie en op­spo­ring on dis­rup­tion measures, 2009

The passage

“In addition to the collection of data, an agent may be charged with promoting or taking measures to protect the interests which the service is required to safeguard (art. 21(1)(a)(2) Wiv 2002). Strictly speaking this is not an activity that falls under the term ‘data processing’, but it concerns the power to take, or to have taken, preventive measures aimed at discouraging or nipping in the bud certain anti-democratic activities, activities dangerous to the State, or other activities directed against the interests named in the Wiv 2002. These disruption measures consist, for example, of obtrusively following or approaching targets, spreading disinformation, or frustrating intended violent actions. A special procedure applies to disruption.” Page 382 of the handbook, reproduced on page 1 of the PDF. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Naast het verzamelen van gegevens, kan een agent worden belast met het bevorderen of treffen van maatregelen ter bescherming van door de dienst te behartigen belangen (art. 21 lid 1 onder a, onder 2 Wiv 2002). Dit is strikt genomen geen activiteit die onder het begrip ‘gegevensverwerking’ valt, maar betreft de bevoegdheid om preventieve maatregelen te (doen) treffen met als doel het ontmoedigen of in de kiem smoren van bepaalde antidemocratische, staatsgevaarlijke of andere activiteiten die zijn gericht tegen de belangen genoemd in de Wiv 2002. Deze verstoringsmaatregelen bestaan bijvoorbeeld in het hinderlijk volgen of het aanspreken van targets, het verspreiden van desinformatie of het frustreren van voorgenomen gewelddadige acties. Voor het verstoren geldt een speciale procedure.” The printed text breaks four words across lines (ge-nomen, antidemocrati-sche, belan-gen, bijvoor-beeld); those breaks have been closed up here. The superscript footnote marker 782 after “speciale procedure” is not reproduced. The source line under the photograph names the authors as “Mr. D. van der Bel, mr. A.M. van Hoorn en mr. J.J.T.M. Pieters”.

Why this document matters

This 2009 handbook for investigative practice explains that an agent may be tasked with taking preventive measures aimed at discouraging or nipping in the bud anti-democratic activities, activities dangerous to the State, or other activities directed against the interests named in the Wiv 2002. As examples of such disruption measures, the handbook lists obtrusively following or approaching targets, spreading disinformation, and frustrating intended violent actions. The essay cites this page for the three grounds on which an intelligence service may take disruption measures, and for the example of disinformation. According to the essay, what has happened to Giltay since 1998 bears the hallmarks of such measures, while none of the three grounds has ever been invoked against him.
