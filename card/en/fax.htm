Exhibit · Fax

Barbara Over­duyn to the National Ombuds­man, signed fax, 16 September 1999

The passage

“Following your letter of 14 September, I have tried several times today to reach you by telephone. Unfortunately there was no answer on the extension you gave. (070-3563599). And I shall be on holiday from Friday 17 September until 3 October. After my holiday I shall contact you at once. Hoping to have informed you sufficiently. Kind regards, Barbara Overduijn” Page 1, the body of the fax with the sign-off beneath it. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Na aanleiding van uw brief d.d. 14 september jl. Heb ik vandaag diverse keren getracht u telefonisch te bereiken. Helaas kreeg ik op het door u opgegeven toestelnummer geen gehoor. (070-3563599). En ben ik vanaf vrijdag 17 september tot 3 oktober as op vakantie. Na mijn vakantie neem ik meteen contact met u op. Hopende u voldoende te hebben geinformeerd. Met vriendelijke groet, Barbara Overduijn” The spellings ‘geinformeerd’ without a diaeresis and ‘as’ for ‘aanstaande’ are the writer’s own, as is the capital in ‘jl. Heb ik’.

Why this document matters

In this fax of 16 September 1999 to the National Ombudsman, Barbara Overduyn signs off as ‘Barbara Overduijn’. The spelling ‘Overduin’ in the book had been taken from the Military Intelligence Service (MID). The essay recalls that the claimant reproached Giltay for a spelling error in her name, and that this fax then surfaced, proving in black and white that she had misspelled her own name. For preliminary relief judge M.E. Groeneveld-Stubbe the spelling of her name was one of the inaccuracies that raised questions about the author’s care, and thus part of the grounds for the full ban. In April 2016, however, the Court of Appeal of The Hague held that those inaccuracies, an age and the spelling of a name, were insufficient to cast doubt on the care with which the author had written the book.

Dateline
The typed dateline reads ‘Den Haag, 16 oktober 1999’, which cannot be right. The transmission line at the top of the page gives 16/09, DON (Thursday), 10:45. The National Ombudsman date-stamped the fax on 16 SEP 1999, and the text itself answers a letter of 14 September and announces a holiday starting on Friday 17 September
