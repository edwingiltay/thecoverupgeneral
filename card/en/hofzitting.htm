TPTV, clip from the appeal hearing, 25 February 2016

The passage

“The case concerns a book whose contents are prohibited from cover to cover. And that, as you know, is extraordinary. In our democratic society, banning an entire book is practically non-existent.” The complete text spoken in the clip, as given in the English subtitles that accompany it. The speaker is Jurian van Groenendaal, Giltay’s lawyer, who is on screen at the start and the end of the clip; the author sits beside him. In between, the three judges are on screen. The original Dutch wording is below.

Original wording

“De zaak gaat over een boek waarvan de inhoud van kaft tot kaft verboden is. En dat is zoals u weet bijzonder. In onze democratische samenleving komt het verbieden van een geheel boek vrijwel niet voor.” The spoken Dutch, as in the Dutch subtitle track of the clip on Vimeo.

Why this document matters

The essay states that on appeal Giltay defended himself with media lawyer Jurian van Groenendaal at his side, after which the Court of Appeal overturned the ban in 2016. This clip shows that hearing. TPTV, the video platform of ThePostOnline, covered the hearing of 25 February 2016 at the Court of Appeal of The Hague in its three-part report ‘Het verboden boek’ (The forbidden book), published on 11 April 2016, the day before the judgment. In the clip Van Groenendaal tells the court that the book is banned from cover to cover and that such a ban practically never occurs in a democratic society. On screen are the three judges who gave the judgment of 12 April 2016: G. Dulek-Schermers on the left, P.H. Blok in the middle and H.J.M. Burg on the right.
