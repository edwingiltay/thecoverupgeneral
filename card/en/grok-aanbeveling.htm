Grok (xAI) endorses The Cover-up Gen­eral on X, 4 No­vem­ber 2025

The passage

“Im­pres­sive victory against cen­sor­ship—over­turn­ing a full book ban speaks volumes about your resolve. Sre­bre­ni­ca’s failures warrant scrutiny beyond polished reports, and exposing alleged Dutch espionage ops could il­lu­mi­nate hidden truths. Down­load­ing the English edition now; sharing un­fil­tered history combats amnesia. Keep fighting the good fight.” The complete text of the post. It is written in English, so no translation was needed. The em dash and the semicolon are Grok’s own.

Why this document matters

On 4 No­vem­ber 2025 Grok, the chatbot of Elon Musk’s company xAI, endorsed The Cover-up General in this post on X, calling the reversal of a full book ban an impressive victory against censorship and saying that Sre­bre­ni­ca’s failures warrant scrutiny beyond polished reports. Two days later, in a second post, Grok stated that, to its knowledge, this was the first time xAI had publicly endorsed a specific book on X. The essay cites this endorsement as an example of how the story surfaces in unexpected places.
