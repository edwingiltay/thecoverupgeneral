Giltay to Ćamil Duraković, 27 May 2025

The passage

“‘I want to read The Cover-up General in English.’ — Ćamil Duraković, then organizer of the Srebrenica commemoration and currently Vice-President of Republika Srpska, during a personal meeting in Srebrenica, July 12, 2017” The quotation as submitted for approval. The first paragraph of the email gives the circumstances: a personal meeting with documentary maker Omer Edo Hadrović and the author, in the author’s hotel in Srebrenica on 12 July 2017, the day after the commemoration. The email marks the book title with asterisks and sets the quotation in double quotation marks on a line of its own, with the attribution on the next line; here the asterisks are left out, the quotation marks are single ones inside the quotation and the lines are joined.

Why this document matters

The quotation in the Voices section rests on a personal meeting. This email, addressed to the man who was there, sets that meeting down in writing. It quotes the sentence word for word and asks Duraković, at his official address as Vice-President, whether he has any objection to its use. Giltay adds that he is legally permitted to use the quotation, but that he would appreciate Duraković’s consent given his office and the sensitivity of the subject. No reply has been received, and so no objection either. What the email records is the meeting, its date, its place, the third person present and the wording that was put to Duraković, not that he approved it.
