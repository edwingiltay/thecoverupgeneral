Min­is­try of De­fence, Wob decisions on the costs of the state attorney, 2 De­cem­ber 2021 and 5 January 2022

From the decision of 2 December 2021

“The second overview, with file number 10001888, contains both the invoices in the Nuhanovic and Mustafic cases, the invoices for the cassation proceedings excepted, and those (from June 2007) in the cases of the Mothers of Srebrenica Foundation et al. Under this file number, several (extensive) sets of proceedings have been handled since the end of 2003 that concerned the liability of the State for the consequences of the fall of the Srebrenica enclave.” Page 1, third paragraph. The line breaks have been closed up here. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

“Het tweede overzicht met het dossiernummer 10001888 bevat zowel de facturen van de zaken Nuhanovic en Mustafic, de facturen van de cassatieprocedure uitgezonderd, als die (vanaf juni 2007) van de zaken van de Stichting Mothers of Srebrenica c.s. Onder dit dossiernummer zijn vanaf eind 2003 meerdere (omvangrijke) procedures behandeld die betrekking hadden op de aansprakelijkheid van de Staat voor de gevolgen van de val van de enclave Srebrenica.” Original wording

From the decision of 5 January 2022

“In response to your request, I decide to make public the remaining costs of the state attorney in the Mothers of Srebrenica case as well. This concerns three invoice overviews, two of which relate to appeals in cassation from the periods 2011 up to and including 2012 and 2017 up to and including 2019. The third invoice overview (file number 10001888) covers the period 2016 up to and including 2021 and concerns the remaining invoices in the Mothers of Srebrenica case.” Page 9 of the PDF, third paragraph. The line breaks have been closed up here. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

“In antwoord op uw verzoek besluit ik ook de overige kosten van de Landsadvocaat in de zaak Mothers of Srebrenica openbaar te maken. Het gaat hier om een drietal factuuroverzichten, waarvan twee betrekking hebben op cassatieberoepen uit de periodes 2011 tot en met 2012 en 2017 tot en met 2019. Het derde factuuroverzicht (dossiernummer 10001888) ziet op de periode 2016 tot en met 2021 en betreft de resterende facturen in de zaak Mothers of Srebrenica.” Original wording

Why this document matters

On 2 December 2021 and 5 January 2022 the Ministry of Defence, at Giltay’s request, released five invoice overviews of the state attorney, covering the cases of Nuhanović and Mustafić and of the Mothers of Srebrenica Foundation. The final totals, including VAT, are €289,555.13 (cassation in Nuhanović and Mustafić, 2011–2013), €1,839,041.87 (the main file, 2003–2015), €39,415.27 and €131,390.65 (cassation in the Mothers’ case, 2011–2012 and 2017–2019) and €327,218.35 (the main file, 2016–2021). Together that is €2,626,621.27. That sum does not appear in the decisions; the essay summarises it as over 2.6 million euros between 2003 and 2021. Balkan Insight reported on it on 21 January 2022.
