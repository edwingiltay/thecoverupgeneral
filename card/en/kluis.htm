National Ombuds­man on the Giltay file, 18 May 2017

The Voices section quotes two statements from this note: that the file is a secret one kept in the safe, and that Defence would have to be asked for its position before more was disclosed. They are set out below in the order of the note.

A secret file in the safe

“File 1999.07789 has the status of departmental secret and not secret, although the complaint also concerned the Military Intelligence Service (now MIVD). The applicant recently submitted a request for review (H201700015). That was rejected with a standard letter. I cannot find the request and the rejection in Verseon. Now, however, it is a secret file in the safe, because of the involvement of the MIVD.” Page 1, the paragraph beginning ‘Dossier 1999.07789 heeft de status’. Verseon is the digital system in which the Ombudsman’s office registers its correspondence, cases and files. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

“Dossier 1999.07789 heeft de status van dienstgeheim en niet geheim, hoewel de klacht mede ging over de Militaire Inlichtingendienst (nu MIVD). Verzoeker heeft recent een herzieningsverzoek ingediend (H201700015). Dat is met een standaardbrief afgewezen. Het verzoek en de afwijzing vind ik niet in Verseon. Nu is het wel een geheim dossier in de kluis, in verband met de betrokkenheid van de MIVD.” Original wording

Defence would have to be asked for its position

“If we were to release more information, we would, in my opinion, have to ask the Ministry of Defence for its position” Page 2, the paragraph beginning ‘Zouden we wel meer informatie’. The sentence has no full stop in the note. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

“Zouden we wel meer informatie ter inzage geven, dan zouden we m.i. Defensie om een zienswijze moeten vragen” Original wording

Why this document matters

In this internal note of 18 May 2017 a staff member at the National Ombudsman writes that Giltay’s file is formally a departmental secret, yet is kept as a “secret file in the safe”, “because of the involvement of the MIVD”. The essay states that the Ombudsman had literally put it away “in the safe”. The staff member also cannot find Giltay’s review request of January 2017 and its rejection in Verseon, the office’s registration system. The same note weighs whether more of the file may be shown, and the staff member takes the view that in that case the Ministry of Defence would first have to be asked for its position.
