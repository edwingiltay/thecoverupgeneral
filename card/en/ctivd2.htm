CTIVD to Giltay, 27 Oc­to­ber 2009

The passage

“In answer to your letter of 24 October 2009 I inform you that the Com­mit­tee, having also taken into account your cor­re­spond­ence with (the State Sec­re­tary of) De­fence, can find no reason to take up your request.” Page 1, the body of the letter, which is a single paragraph. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“In antwoord op uw schrijven van 24 oktober 2009 deel ik u mede dat de Commissie, mede in aanmerking genomen uw briefwisseling met (de Staatssecretaris van) Defensie, geen aanleiding kan vinden op uw verzoek in te gaan.”

Why this document matters

Writing on 27 Oc­to­ber 2009, the chair of the CTIVD, A.H. van Delden, tells Giltay that the com­mit­tee, having also taken Giltay’s cor­re­spond­ence with De­fence into account, “can find no reason” to take up his request. He had succeeded I.P. Michiels van Kessenich-Hoogendam, who as chair had written to Giltay in January of the same year. These two letters from successive chairs form the basis of the essay’s statement that the CTIVD investigation, the second pillar of Defence’s 2017 refusal, did not exist, and that this had been on the record since 2009.
