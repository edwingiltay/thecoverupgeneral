Radio-televizija Srbije on the Dutch ban, 24 De­cem­ber 2015

The passage

“In the book, which received a good review from former Minister Jan Pronk, centre stage is taken by the conflict between two factions within the Dutch Ministry of Defence in the dramatic days after the crime in Srebrenica. One side wanted at all costs to destroy the film roll, compromising to the Dutch government, on which Serb crimes against the Bosniaks of Srebrenica at the battalion’s base were recorded, while the other held that it should nevertheless be made available to the wider public, Avaz reports.” The fourth and fifth paragraphs of the article, directly below the paragraph that credits the Sarajevo daily Dnevni avaz. Trans­lat­ed from the Ser­bi­an; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“У књизи, која је добила добру рецензију бившег министра Јана Пронка, централно место заузима сукоб између две стране у холандском Министарству одбране у драматичним данима након злочина у Сребреници. Једна струја је по сваку цену хтела да уништити за холандску владу компромитујућу фото-ролну где су забележени српски злочини над сребреничким Бошњацима у бази батаљона, док је друга сматрала да она ипак треба бити доступна широкој јавности, наводи Аваз.” Quoted from the article as published on rts.rs in its original Cyrillic-script edition, where the two sentences appear as separate paragraphs. The broadcaster sets the name Аваз (Avaz) in italics. The wording ‘хтела да уништити’ is the broadcaster’s own; standard Serbian would have ‘хтела да уништи’.

Why this document matters

The ban did not stay a Dutch matter. Ten days after the judgment the Serbian public broad­cast­er reported that a court in The Hague had forbidden Edwin Giltay to distribute and sell his book about the Dutch UN battalion in Sre­bre­ni­ca, adding that the book had already been on sale for a year and that Dutch media were shocked that it could be banned in a country of proclaimed freedoms. Citing the Sarajevo daily Dnevni avaz as its source, RTS then set out what the book is about: the conflict between two factions within the Dutch Min­is­try of De­fence in the days after the genocide in Sre­bre­ni­ca.
