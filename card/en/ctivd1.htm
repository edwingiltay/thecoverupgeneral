Exhibit · Letter

CTIVD to Giltay, 12 January 2009

The passage

“To date the Committee has not received from the Minister of Defence a request for advice on your complaint. The Committee is therefore unable at this time to investigate your complaint further.” Page 1, the paragraph beginning ‘Tot op heden’. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Tot op heden heeft de Commissie een verzoek om advies over uw klacht van de Minister van Defensie niet ontvangen. Derhalve kan de Commissie op dit moment uw klacht niet nader onderzoeken.” Line breaks in the scan have been removed.

Why this document matters

In June 2017 the Min­is­try of Defence refused to retract the 1999 min­is­te­ri­al declaration of insanity, citing an Om­buds­man report and a CTIVD in­ves­ti­ga­tion that were said to have found Giltay’s com­plaints about the MIVD unfounded. The essay sets out that the CTIVD investigation, the second pillar, did not exist, and that this had been on the record since 2009.

This letter is the first of the two documents the essay points to: the committee writes that to date no request for advice on the complaint has reached it from the Minister of Defence, and that it cannot therefore investigate the complaint further at this time. In the same letter the committee explains that Article 83 of the In­tel­li­gence and Security Services Act 2002 obliges the Min­is­ter to seek its advice whenever he takes up a complaint about the MIVD.

Legal basis cited
Article 83 of the Intelligence and Security Services Act 2002 (Wet op de inlichtingen- en veiligheidsdiensten 2002) and the complaints procedure of the General Administrative Law Act (Algemene wet bestuursrecht), under which the Minister of Defence must seek the committee’s advice when he takes up a complaint about the MIVD. The letter adds that under Article 9:8(1) of the General Administrative Law Act the Minister may decide not to handle a complaint, among other grounds where the conduct complained of was the subject of an earlier complaint already dealt with, or took place more than a year before the complaint was lodged
