Com­mit­tee on the In­tel­li­gence and Se­cu­rity Ser­vices, report for 2010, 16 May 2011

The passage

“The composition of the CIVD after the first sitting of the newly elected House of Representatives was: Rutte (VVD), chair of the committee, Cohen (PvdA), Wilders (PVV), Verhagen (CDA), Roemer (SP), Pechtold (D66), Halsema (GroenLinks), Rouvoet (ChristenUnie), Thieme (PvdD) and Van der Staaij (SGP). After the swearing-in of the Rutte cabinet on 14 October 2010, Blok (VVD) replaced Rutte as chair of the committee because the latter became Prime Minister, and Van Haersma Buma (CDA) replaced Verhagen, who was appointed Minister of Economic Affairs, Agriculture and Innovation (ELI).” Page 2, under the heading ‘Samenstelling van de commissie’ (Composition of the committee). Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“De samenstelling van de CIVD na de eerste samenkomst van de nieuw gekozen Tweede Kamer was: Rutte (VVD), voorzitter van de commissie, Cohen (PvdA), Wilders (PVV), Verhagen (CDA), Roemer (SP), Pechtold (D66), Halsema (GroenLinks), Rouvoet (ChristenUnie), Thieme (PvdD) en Van der Staaij (SGP). Na de beëdiging van het kabinet-Rutte op 14 oktober 2010 heeft Blok (VVD) Rutte als voorzitter van de commissie vervangen omdat laatstgenoemde minister-president werd en Van Haersma Buma (CDA) Verhagen, die tot minister van Economische Zaken, Landbouw en Innovatie (ELI) is benoemd.” The line breaks have been closed up here.

Why this document matters

This report for 2010 gives the composition of the Committee on the Intelligence and Security Services, which consists of the leaders of the parliamentary groups in the House of Representatives. After the elections of June 2010 its members included Cohen (PvdA) and Wilders (PVV), and after the swearing-in of the Rutte cabinet on 14 October 2010 Blok (VVD) replaced Rutte as chair. Cohen, Wilders and Blok were therefore all members when, on 20 November 2010, Giltay sent them his confidential letter with a witness report. The committee’s report is signed by Blok as chair. It says nothing about that letter.
