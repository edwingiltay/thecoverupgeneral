District Court of The Hague, judgment of 27 December 2023 (ECLI:­NL:­RBDHA:­2023:­20409)

The passage

“The court: declares the appeal against the failure to take a timely decision on the request well-founded; annuls the failure to take a timely decision, which is to be equated with a decision; orders the respondent to take a decision on the applicant’s request of 1 September 2023 within two weeks of the day on which this judgment is sent; determines that the respondent shall forfeit to the applicant a penalty payment of €100 for each day by which he exceeds the aforementioned period, up to a maximum of €15,000;” The operative part of the judgment (‘Beslissing’) on page 3, the first four orders, set out in the judgment as a bulleted list. Trans­lat­ed from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“De rechtbank: verklaart het beroep tegen het niet tijdig nemen van een besluit op het verzoek gegrond; vernietigt het met een besluit gelijk te stellen niet tijdig nemen van een besluit; draagt verweerder op, binnen twee weken na de dag van verzending van deze uitspraak, alsnog een besluit te nemen op het verzoek van eiser van 1 september 2023; bepaalt dat verweerder aan eiser een dwangsom van € 100,- verbeurt voor elke dag waarmee hij de hiervoor genoemde termijn overschrijdt, met een maximum van € 15.000,-;” ‘Eiser’, the applicant, is Giltay; ‘verweerder’, the respondent, is the National Ombudsman. The version published on deeplink.rechtspraak.nl/uitspraak?id=ECLI:NL:RBDHA:2023:20409 anonymises the applicant as [eiser] and the report number 1999/507 as [nummer]; the copy on this site names both.

Why this document matters

This is the second of the two 2023 judgments in which the court ruled against the National Ombudsman over an information request. Having already found that the institution had wrongly blocked an earlier request, the court held here that the Ombudsman had also failed to decide in time on another Woo request, of 1 September 2023. It ordered him to do so within two weeks, and attached a penalty payment of 100 euros for every day by which he exceeded that deadline, up to 15,000 euros. Giltay used the penalty payments imposed on the Ombudsman to finance the English translation of his book, which he then made available worldwide free of charge.
