Ministry of De­fence to the Na­tion­al Om­buds­man, 2 March 1999

The passage

“For the sake of good order I would draw your at­ten­tion to the fol­low­ing. On 26 Jan­u­a­ry 1999, in con­nec­tion with this case, a meeting took place at the MID between one of your staff, Mr P.P.F. Schets, the Chief of Staff of the MID and a member of the Di­rec­to­rate of Legal Affairs, Mr J.W. Se­ve­rij­nen. It was agreed there that the MID would con­duct an in­ter­nal in­ves­ti­ga­tion into the alleged in­volve­ment of Mrs B. Over­duin and Captain (Navy) W.H.C. van Straten in this matter. In ac­cor­dance with that ar­range­ment, in­ter­views were held with these persons. A report of this in­ves­ti­ga­tion is en­closed as annex 1. The results speak for them­selves.” Page 1, second paragraph. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Voor de goede orde vraag ik uw aan­dacht nog voor het vol­gen­de. Op 26 ja­nu­a­ri 1999 heeft in het ka­der van deze zaak bij de MID een ge­sprek plaats­ge­von­den tus­sen één van uw me­de­wer­kers, de heer P.P.F. Schets, de Chef Staf van de MID en een me­de­wer­ker van de Di­rec­tie Ju­ri­di­sche Za­ken, de heer mr. drs. J.W. Se­ve­rij­nen. Hier­bij werd af­ge­spro­ken dat er door de MID een in­tern on­der­zoek zou wor­den ge­hou­den naar de ver­meen­de be­trok­ken­heid van me­vrouw B. Over­duin en de KTZ W.H.C. van Stra­ten bij deze kwes­tie. Met deze per­so­nen heb­ben con­form de ge­maak­te af­spraak ge­sprek­ken plaats­ge­von­den. Een ver­slag van dit on­der­zoek treft u in bij­la­ge 1 aan. De re­sul­ta­ten daar­van spre­ken voor zich.” The letter spells the name “Overduin”; elsewhere in the file it appears as “Overduyn”.

Why this document matters

After Giltay filed a com­plaint with the Na­tion­al Om­buds­man, the MID em­ploy­ee called him “ir­ri­ta­ting”, “mal­ad­just­ed” and “com­plete­ly insane”. She made those state­ments in an in­ves­ti­ga­tion the MID opened into itself, under an arrangement recorded in this letter. That arrangement was agreed on 26 Jan­u­a­ry 1999 between the Chief of Staff of the ser­vice, who was also its deputy head, the Om­buds­man’s case officer and an official of the Directorate of Legal Affairs of the Ministry of Defence. The Min­is­ter of De­fence attached the report of that self-investigation as annex 1 to this letter, his written response to the Ombudsman, with the remark that the results “speak for themselves”.
