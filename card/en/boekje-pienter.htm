Boekje Pienter, five-star review, 2017

The passage

“In ‘De doofpotgeneraal’ nothing is what it seems. It is almost suffocating to read how the intelligence services (or was it the general himself?) band together to make life miserable for innocent citizens. And how tribal wars are fought within the intelligence services. […] Such a cover-up must be smothered.” Seventh of the twelve paragraphs of the review, and the closing sentence of the review. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“In ‘De doofpotgeneraal’ lijkt niets wat het is. Het is bijna verstikkend om te lezen hoe de inlichtingendiensten - of was het de generaal zelf? - te hoop lopen om onschuldige burgers het leven zuur te maken. En hoe er binnen de inlichtingendiensten stammenstrijden plaatsvinden. […] Zo’n doofpot moet gesmoord worden.” Quoted from the page as archived on 18 August 2017. The review sets the interjection between plain hyphens; the English rendering puts it in brackets. The book title appears in single quotation marks in the original.

Why this document matters

The Voices section counts this review among the star reviews the book received. Boekje Pienter, a Dutch military reading guide, gives De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal five stars out of five, the highest mark in the guide’s own key, where five stars stand for ‘Absoluut lezen’ (absolutely worth reading). The reviewer identifies the general of the title as Ad van Baal, who was deputy commander of the army when Srebrenica fell in 1995, and calls the book almost suffocating to read. The reviewer asks what became of the now world-famous Dutchbat roll of film, and the review closes with the line that such a cover-up must be smothered.
