Dis­trict Court of The Hague, advance notice of the hearing in Giltay’s Woo appeals, 28 Sep­tem­ber 2026

The passage

“The court intends to hear your appeal on the morning of 24 November 2026, in the court building in The Hague.” Page 1, under the heading ‘Vooraankondiging’ (advance notice). The same sentence appears in the letter on page 2. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“De rechtbank is voornemens uw beroep op 24 november 2026 in de ochtend te behandelen, in het gerechtsgebouw te Den Haag.” Line breaks in the scan have been closed up.

Why this document matters

In this letter of 28 September 2026, the registrar of the District Court of The Hague announces that the court intends to hear Giltay’s appeal with case number SGR 24 / 191 WOO on 24 November 2026. WOO stands for the Wet open overheid, the Open Government Act, which governs the right of access to government documents. An identically worded letter of the same date, page 2 of the PDF, announces the hearing of a second appeal, SGR 24 / 7604 WOO, for the same morning. The letters show that in 2026 Giltay is litigating before the administrative court to have government documents made public. They say nothing about the substance or the outcome of the cases.
