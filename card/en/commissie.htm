Standing Committee on Defence to Giltay, 18 September 2017

The passage

“Your abovementioned letter was considered on 14 September 2017 by the Standing Committee on Defence at a procedural meeting. At that meeting the committee decided to ask the Minister of Defence for a response to your letter. The committee will send you that response once it has been received. It will then also let you know whether it wishes to take any further steps and, if so, which ones.” Page 1, the body of the letter, immediately below the salutation. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Uw bovengenoemde brief is op 14 september 2017 door de vaste commissie voor Defensie in een procedurevergadering behandeld. In die vergadering heeft de commissie besloten de minister van Defensie een reactie te vragen op uw brief. De commissie zal deze reactie na ontvangst aan u toesturen. Dan zal zij u ook laten weten of zij nog nadere stappen wil ondernemen en zo ja, welke.” Line breaks in the scan have been removed and the three paragraphs run together.

Why this document matters

On 1 June 2017 Giltay asked the House of Representatives for clarification about the Srebrenica cover-up affair. The Standing Committee on Defence discussed his letter at a procedural meeting on 14 September 2017. It unanimously decided to ask the Minister of Defence for a response. In this letter it undertook to forward that response to Giltay. The Minister replied to the House on 13 October 2017. On 9 November 2017 the committee sent Giltay the reply and took note of it. That Letter to Parliament addresses the film roll and the claim that Defence had tried to ‘psychiatrise’ Giltay. But the MID and Casema do not appear in it. No substantive response to the intelligence affair has ever come.
