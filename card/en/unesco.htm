UNESCO Char­ter on the Pres­er­va­tion of the Dig­i­tal Her­i­tage, 17 October 2003

The passage

“Member States need appropriate legal and institutional frameworks to secure the protection of their digital heritage. As a key element of national preservation policy, archive legislation and legal or voluntary deposit in libraries, archives, museums and other public repositories should embrace the digital heritage.” Article 8, ‘Protecting the digital heritage’, the two opening paragraphs on printed page 3 (page 4 of the five-page PDF). The paragraph break between the two sentences has been closed up here.

Why this document matters

The UNESCO char­ter counts web pages among the dig­i­tal her­i­tage, and asks Member States to extend archive leg­is­la­tion and legal or voluntary deposit in libraries, archives and mu­se­ums to that her­i­tage. It adds that legal and technical frameworks for au­then­tic­i­ty are crucial to prevent ma­nip­u­la­tion or intentional al­ter­a­tion of dig­i­tal her­i­tage. The essay states that in June 2026 the national library KB selected this website for its web col­lec­tion in line with this char­ter, and that from 12 July 2026 the site was archived, beyond the author’s reach, in the Depot of Dutch Pub­li­ca­tions.
