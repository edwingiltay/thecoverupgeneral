Al Jazeera Docu­men­tary photo story on In­sta­gram, 22 April 2025

The passage

“The Srebrenica massacre, which killed some 8,000 Muslims in Bosnia, still causes controversy today, but a Dutch book banned 10 years ago sheds light on other dimensions of the case. What is its story?” The post’s caption, below the slides, in full apart from the two closing hashtags. Trans­lat­ed from the Arabic; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“ما تزال مجزرة سربرنيتسا التي قتلت نحو 8 آلاف مسلم في البوسنة تثير الجدل حتى الآن، لكن كتابا هولنديا حُظر قبل 10 سنوات يروي أبعادا أخرى في القضية. فما قصته؟” The caption is written in Arabic. It spells Srebrenica as سربرنيتسا. The two hashtags that close the caption are left out of the quotation.

Why this document matters

On 22 April 2025, in ten slides, Al Jazeera Documentary told its Arabic-speaking audience the story of a Dutch book on Srebrenica banned in 2015: the espionage affair inside the military intelligence service, the missing photographs, the ban and its lifting on appeal. The channel also noted that the Dutch parliament took up the book in 2017.
