Naša Bosna on the meeting with Am­bas­sa­dor Čolaković, 17 April 2018

The passage

“Mr Edwin Giltay, author of the initially banned book ‘De Doofpotgeneraal’, which tells of the concealment of rolls of film with images of the massacre at Srebrenica, presented Ambassador Čolaković with the second edition of his book, for which she thanked him warmly and expressed the wish that the book be translated into Bosnian as well.” Fourth paragraph of the report, page 10 of the issue. Translated from the Bosnian; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“Gospodin Edwin Giltay, autor prvobitno zabranjene knjige ‘De Doofpotgeneraal’, koja govori o skrivanju fotorolni sa snimcima masakra u Srebrenici, je poklonio ambasadorici Čolaković drugo izdanje svoje knjige, na ćemu se ona srdačno zahvalila i izrazila želju da se knjiga prevede i na Bosanski jezik.” The article is in Bosnian.

Why this document matters

This report in Naša Bosna, the paper for citizens of Bosnia and Herzegovina in the Netherlands, records the working visit to the embassy of Bosnia and Herzegovina in The Hague on 16 April 2018, during which Edwin Giltay presented Ambassador Mirsada Čolaković with the second edition of the initially banned book. She thanked him and expressed the wish that it be translated into Bosnian as well. The report thus confirms her quotation in the Voices section, words she spoke to him in person. He attended as one of the representatives of the committee preparing the Srebrenica commemoration. The essay lists Naša Bosna among the publications that wrote about the case.
