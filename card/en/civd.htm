Exhibit · Letter

Giltay to three members of the CIVD, 20 No­vem­ber 2010

The passage

“I have set down my experiences with Dutch military intelligence in a witness report. I enclose this report. As a citizen I consider it important that you, as a member of the ‘commissie Stiekem’, should be properly informed about this.” Page 2, the second paragraph of the letter to S. Blok. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. The ‘commissie Stiekem’, literally the secret committee, is the everyday Dutch name for the Committee on the Intelligence and Security Services.

Original wording

“Mijn ervaringen met het Nederlandse militaire inlichtingenwezen heb ik opgetekend in een getuigenverslag. Hierbij zend ik U dit verslag. Als burger acht ik het van belang dat U als lid van de commissie Stiekem hierover goed wordt ingelicht.” The same paragraph appears in the letters to Cohen (page 1) and Wilders (page 3).

Why this document matters

On 20 No­vem­ber 2010 Giltay addressed this confidential letter to the parliamentary group leaders Job Cohen, Stef Blok and Geert Wil­ders, who at the time also sat on the Com­mit­tee on the In­tel­li­gence and Se­cu­rity Ser­vices, known in The Hague as the ‘com­mis­sie Stiekem’. En­closed was a detailed witness report of the affair, an early manuscript version of The Cover-up Gen­eral. He wrote that as a citizen he considered it important that the committee be properly informed. Giltay never learned what happened to the letter after it was received.
