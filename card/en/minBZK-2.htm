Ministry of the Interior to Giltay, 17 April 2015

The passage

“In view of the foregoing I have decided after all not to consider the second part of your complaint, since it concerns conduct that took place more than a year before the complaint was lodged and of which you were, moreover, aware more than a year before the complaint was lodged (Article 9:8, first paragraph, under b, of the General Ad­min­is­tra­tive Law Act).” Page 2, the paragraph in which the decision is taken. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Gelet op het voorgaande heb ik alsnog besloten het tweede deel van uw klacht niet in behandeling te nemen nu zij betrekking heeft op een gedraging die langer dan een jaar voor indiening van de klacht heeft plaatsgevonden en u hiervan ook al langer dan een jaar voor indiening van de klacht op de hoogte was (artikel 9:8, eerste lid, onder b, van de Awb).”

Why this document matters

In this letter of 17 April 2015 the Minister of the Interior adopts the advice of the CTIVD, the Review Committee on the Intelligence and Security Services, and declares unfounded the first part of Giltay’s complaint, about his suspicion that the AIVD had intercepted his cor­re­spond­ence with the lawyer Britta Böhler about the manuscript. The Minister refuses to consider the second part, on the affair described in The Cover-up Gen­er­al, because the conduct complained of took place more than a year before the complaint. The Ministry therefore passes no judgment on the affair itself. The essay states that the Minister, Ronald Plas­terk, having declined to respond substantively to the manuscript, none the less had the CTIVD investigate the author’s complaint about the AIVD, and withdrew that investigation forty-six days later, with this letter. When De­fence gave reasons in June 2017 for refusing to withdraw the declaration of insanity, it invoked a CTIVD investigation said to have concerned Giltay’s complaints about the MIVD. This letter shows that in 2015 the CTIVD investigated only a complaint about the AIVD.
