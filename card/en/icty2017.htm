ICTY Office of the Prose­cu­tor to Giltay, 3 October 2017

The passage

“We don’t have knowledge of 8 photo rolls that you refer to as being under lock and key in military intelligence archives.” Page 1, first sentence of the fourth paragraph of the email. The document is in English.

Why this document matters

Under Resolution 827, adopted under Chapter VII of the UN Charter, every state, the Netherlands included, had to cooperate fully with the tribunal and comply with its requests for assistance and its orders. Giltay put the question of the rolls of film to the tribunal, and this is the reply he received. The Office of the Prosecutor states that it has no knowledge of the eight rolls described as being under lock and key in military intelligence archives. Giltay took the number eight from the ANP report of 28 November 2016 on ThePostOnline, in which the lawyers Klaas Arjen Krikke and Michael Ruperti, acting for more than two hundred Dutchbat III veterans, stated that prints of at least eight rolls of film were being withheld in the MIVD archive.
