Giltay to Munira Su­ba­šić, 20 May 2026

The passage

“On 27 June 2017, we met at the Palace of Justice in The Hague, shortly before the Court of Appeal’s ruling in the case brought by your association against the Dutch State. Journalist Naida Ribić made the introduction and translated.” The opening paragraph of the letter. The book was enclosed with it. The letter does not mention the flower Subašić handed over at that meeting.

Why this document matters

This letter, addressed to the woman who was there, sets down in writing the personal meeting on which the quotation in the Voices section rests. It names the date, the place, the occasion and the journalist who made the introduction and translated, the Bosnian-Dutch Naida Ribić. What it does not do is repeat the words themselves. The Srebrenica flower that Subašić handed over is not mentioned in the letter; the quotation rests on the author’s account of a meeting that is in other respects documented. The words quoted are the translation Naida Ribić gave on the spot.
