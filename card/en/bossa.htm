The passage

“From left to right: Zambian interviewer Esnart Soeters, Ugandan judge Solomy Balungi Bossa of the Appeals Chamber of the International Criminal Court in The Hague, and singers Talya and Arjen. Event: Fallen | Cancelled | Mercy?, a debate hosted by the American Protestant Church in The Hague and LUX Academy, in cultural centre Amare, The Hague. Photo: Edwin Giltay, Wednesday evening 25 March 2026” Page 1, the caption printed below the photograph. The document is in English. Its three caption lines, each headed ‘From left to right’, ‘Event’ and ‘Photo’ in italics, are run together here, with a full stop added after ‘The Hague’ at the end of the second.

Why this document matters

The closing section of the essay states that on 25 March 2026 the author spoke with Judge Solomy Balungi Bossa of the International Criminal Court, who took part as the main guest in a public debate at Amare, The Hague, within sight of the Ministry of Defence. This document contains the photograph the author himself took of the panel that evening, with its caption, followed by the announcement that co-organiser LUX Academy posted on Instagram. It shows the date, the venue and the identity of the judge to whom he put his question there about those who cover up evidence of war crimes. Bossa had previously spent ten years as a judge at the UN tribunal for Rwanda, and then served at the Residual Mechanism that continues the work of the Rwanda and Yugoslavia tribunals.
