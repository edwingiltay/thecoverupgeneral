MEP Moritz Körner to Giltay, 11 De­cem­ber 2024

The passage

“Thank you very much for your detailed message and for describing your experiences and assessments regarding Mr Reinier van Zutphen. I share your concern about transparency and integrity in the office of the European Ombudsman. The election of the Ombudsman is an important decision, which we as parliamentarians examine carefully. I will take your examples and suggestions into account in the selection process and bring them into the discussion.” Page 1, the first paragraph of the reply, directly under the salutation. Translated from the German; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“vielen Dank für Ihre ausführliche Nachricht und die Schilderung Ihrer Erfahrungen und Einschätzungen zu Herrn Reinier van Zutphen. Ihre Sorge um Transparenz und Integrität im Amt des Europäischen Bürgerbeauftragten teile ich. Die Wahl der Bürgerbeauftragten ist eine wichtige Entscheidung, die wir als Parlamentarier sorgfältig prüfen. Ihre Beispiele und Hinweise werde ich im Rahmen des Auswahlprozesses berücksichtigen und in die Diskussion einbringen.” The paragraph directly follows the salutation ‘Sehr geehrter Herr Giltay,’, which is why it opens with a lower-case letter. Körner wrote and signed the message himself, from his parliamentary address. A second, short paragraph thanks Giltay for the material he sent and promises to look at the points raised more closely.

Why this document matters

Moritz Körner’s quotation in the Voices section comes from this email. Reinier van Zutphen, then National Ombudsman of the Netherlands, was a candidate in the election of the European Ombudsman on 17 December 2024. On 8 December Giltay wrote to the German liberal MEP to explain why he doubted the candidate’s record on transparency: Giltay had been discredited in an official report of a previous National Ombudsman, R. Fernhout, after witnessing a military intelligence affair connected with the Dutch role in Srebrenica. The Court of Appeal of The Hague had, according to Giltay, since confirmed the accuracy of his book, and Van Zutphen had neither withdrawn nor corrected that report. Giltay asked that these concerns be considered carefully before the vote, and attached the PDF of the English edition of his book. The answer came three days later, from Körner himself: he shares the concern about transparency and integrity in the office, calls the election an important decision that Parliament examines carefully, and promises to take the examples into account in the selection process and raise them in the discussion. He passes no judgement on the candidate himself.
