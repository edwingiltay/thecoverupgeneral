House of Rep­re­sen­ta­tives to the Ob­jec­tions Ad­vi­so­ry Com­mit­tee, ex­pla­na­tion of Woo de­ci­sion W2026005, 14 Oc­to­ber 2026

The passage

“Although the book has had a social impact, has given rise to discussion and has played a role in the proceedings that led to the judgment of the Supreme Court of 19 July 2019 (ECLI:NL:HR:2019:1223), these factors, like the journalistic interest asserted by the applicant, are not interests that count in the balancing of interests under the Woo.” Page 10 of the explanation (page 2 of this PDF). Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Hoewel het boek maatschappelijke impact heeft gehad, tot discussie heeft geleid en een rol heeft gespeeld bij de procedure die leidde tot het arrest van de Hoge Raad van 19 juli 2019 (ECLI:NL:HR:2019:1223), zijn deze factoren, evenals het journalistieke belang zoals verzoeker stelt, geen belangen die op grond van de Woo in de belangenafweging meetellen.” Line breaks in the document have been closed up.

Why this document matters

In this explanation of 14 October 2026, the Legal Affairs Department of the Dutch House of Representatives defends, before the Objections Advisory Committee, the decision of 20 April 2026 on Giltay’s request under the Open Government Act (Wet open overheid, Woo). That request sought documents of the Committee on the Intelligence and Security Services about the affair described in the book; according to the decision, none were found. In this defence the department acknowledges that the book has had a social impact, has given rise to discussion and has played a role in the proceedings that led to the judgment of the Supreme Court of 19 July 2019 in the case of the Mothers of Srebrenica against the State. It adds at once that such factors do not count under the Woo. The PDF contains pages 1, 10 and 11 of the document.
