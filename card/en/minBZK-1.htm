Exhibit · Letter

Ministry of the Interior to Giltay, 2 March 2015

The passage

“I hereby confirm receipt of your supplementary complaint of 11 February 2015 about the AIVD. Your letter has been forwarded to the CTIVD.” Page 2 of the file, the body of the ministry’s letter of 2 March 2015. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Hierbij bevestig ik de ontvangst van uw aanvullende klacht over de AIVD d.d. 11 februari 2015. Uw brief is aan de CTIVD doorgezonden.”

Why this document matters

In this letter of 2 March 2015 the Ministry of the Interior confirms receipt of the supplementary complaint about the AIVD and states that it has been forwarded to the CTIVD. The author’s own letter of 11 February 2015, asking for the whole affair described in De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal to be investigated, is reproduced as the first page of the same file. The essay states that the Minister of the Interior, Ronald Plasterk, declined to respond substantively to the book, but that at the author’s request an official investigation by the CTIVD, the Review Committee on the Intelligence and Security Services, was set in motion. The Minister had instructed the CTIVD to investigate and, the essay notes, withdrew that instruction forty-six days later, on 17 April 2015.
