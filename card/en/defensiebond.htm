Onafhankelijke Defensiebond, book review, 10 May 2021

The passage

“This book offers a disconcerting look into a world where espionage, blackmail and the unauthorised deployment of resources are the order of the day. Giltay reveals matters that cannot bear the light of day. And of course he also answers that one question: who is the general keeping the lid on this cover-up? […] Given the ongoing lawsuits and investigations in the aftermath of the Srebrenica tragedy, this book is undoubtedly of current relevance.” The paragraph under the subheading ‘Onthutsend’ (disconcerting), followed by the last sentence of the paragraph under ‘Actueel’ (topical). The ellipsis […] replaces the subheading ‘Actueel’ and the first two sentences below it. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Dit boek biedt een onthutsende inkijk in een wereld waar spionage, chantage en de ongeoorloofde inzet van middelen aan de orde van de dag zijn. Giltay onthult zaken die het daglicht niet kunnen verdragen. En natuurlijk beantwoordt hij ook die ene vraag: wie is de generaal die het deksel op deze doofpot houdt? […] Gezien de nu lopende rechtszaken en onderzoeken in de nasleep van het Srebrenica-drama, is dit boek zeer beslist van actueel belang.” Quoted from the article as it appears on defensiebond.nl.

Why this document matters

This review in the book section of the On­af­han­ke­lij­ke Defensie­bond, which describes itself as a union for everyone who works or has worked for the Dutch armed forces, opens with the news that De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal may be published again now that the earlier ban has been set aside on appeal. The review, which the union itself credits to De Blauwe Tijger and the ODB editors, says Giltay reveals matters that cannot bear the light of day, and that the book is of undoubted current relevance given the lawsuits and investigations still running in the aftermath of Sre­bre­ni­ca. The essay counts the review among the examples of sustained attention the book drew between 2018 and 2023.
