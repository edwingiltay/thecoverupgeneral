Minister Bij­le­veld to Giltay on Twitter, 25 Oc­to­ber 2018

The passage

“Com­plete­ly right. Too hasty a Re­sponse on my part. It has since been amended. Thank you for your alert re­ac­tion” The Min­is­ter’s reply, the second post in the thread. Trans­lat­ed from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Vol­ko­men ge­lijk. Te snel­le Res­pon­se van mij. In­mid­dels aan­ge­past. Dank voor uw aler­te re­ac­tie” ‘Response’ carries a capital in the original because it is the word Giltay had just corrected: the Benelux ar­range­ment is called Quick Reaction Alert, not ‘Quick Response Alert’. The reply ends without a full stop.

Why this document matters

In 2016 Over­duyn did not challenge in her statement of de­fence that Giltay had used evidence to disprove her claim that he had been dismissed, and in 2017 the Min­is­try of Defence answered Giltay’s request for a re­trac­tion of the declaration of insanity by stating that his case was considered closed. A year later Minister of Defence Ank Bijleveld herself told him in a tweet that he was completely right about the name of a Benelux arrangement, and thanked him for his alertness. In the same thread Giltay asked her to correct the MID report of 12 Feb­ru­a­ry 1999 that called him ‘completely insane’, and to concede that he was right about that too. The 1999 report was not amended.
