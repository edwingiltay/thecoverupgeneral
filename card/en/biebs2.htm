Defence libraries catalogue entries, 23 March 2026

The passage

“Availability of this printing at the Defence libraries. KIM: 1 available at this holding location. NIMH: 1 available at this holding location.” Page 3, the availability panel of the 2014 edition. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Beschikbaarheid voor deze oplage bij Defensiebibliotheken. KIM: 1 beschikbaar op deze opslaglocatie. NIMH: 1 beschikbaar op deze opslaglocatie.” Each library appears on a card of its own, with a tick before the line. The wording above joins the heading and the two cards, and leaves out the search box that sits between them.

Why this document matters

The Ministry of Defence’s own library catalogue shows the book in three Defence libraries: the revised 2016 edition at the Royal Military Academy, and the 2014 first edition at the Royal Naval College and the Netherlands Institute of Military History. These records are what the line in the essay rests on.
