De Andere Krant, book page, 6 May 2020

The passage

“His book about it offers a disconcerting look into a world where espionage, blackmail and the improper deployment of resources are the order of the day. An earlier ban on this publication was thrown out by the Court of Appeal, with the judgment: ‘There is no doubt about the care taken.’” Page 1, the closing sentences of the book page. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Zijn boek hierover biedt een onthutsende inkijk in een wereld waar spionage, chantage en de ongeoorloofde inzet van middelen aan de orde van de dag zijn. Een eerder verbod op deze uitgave werd door het gerechtshof van tafel geveegd met het oordeel: ‘Er bestaat geen twijfel over de zorgvuldigheid.’” The text is set as one continuous paragraph; in the wording above, the line breaks have been closed up.

Why this document matters

In its books section on 6 May 2020 De Andere Krant, a Dutch newspaper, called the book a surprising eye-opener for anyone who thinks of espionage as something remote. The newspaper describes the author as an unwilling witness to infighting inside the Military Intelligence Service, fought out at an office in the polder, with the notorious Sre­bre­ni­ca film roll at stake. The page closes by recording that an earlier ban on the book had been thrown out by the Court of Appeal. The essay counts this page as part of the sustained attention the affair drew between 2018 and 2023.
