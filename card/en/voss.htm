Office of Axel Voss MEP to Giltay, 11 De­cem­ber 2024

The passage

“On behalf of Mr Voss I thank you most sincerely for your message. Mr Voss is aware of the concerns about the candidate and will take them into account in his vote.” Page 1, the body of the reply, directly under the salutation. Translated from the German; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Im Namen von Herrn Voss bedanke ich mich ganz herzlich für Ihre Zuschrift. Herr Voss sind die Bedenken zum Kandidaten bekannt und er wird sie in seiner Wahl berücksichtigen.” The two sentences appear as separate paragraphs in the email, with a blank line between them; the quotation above runs them together. The message was written by a policy adviser and press officer in Voss’s office, in his name, and comes from Voss’s parliamentary address. The staff member’s name and contact details are blacked out in this PDF.

Why this document matters

Axel Voss’s quotation in the Voices section comes from this email. Reinier van Zutphen, then National Ombudsman of the Netherlands, was a candidate in the election of the European Ombudsman on 17 December 2024. On 8 December Giltay wrote to the German Christian Democrat MEP to explain why he doubted the candidate’s record on transparency: Giltay had been discredited in an official report of the then National Ombudsman, R. Fernhout, after witnessing a military intelligence affair connected with the Dutch role in Srebrenica, the Court of Appeal of The Hague had, in his account, since confirmed the accuracy of his book, and Van Zutphen had neither withdrawn nor corrected that report. Giltay asked that these concerns be considered carefully before the vote, and attached the PDF of the English edition of his book. The answer came three days later from Voss’s office, in his name, and is two lines long: the concerns are known to him, and he will take them into account in his vote.
