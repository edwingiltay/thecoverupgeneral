NOS Journaal, 13 July 1999

The passage

“8 p.m. news, presented by Pia Dijkstra, with the following items: Minister De Grave dismisses leadership of military intelligence service MID: statement by De Grave; -*conversation with political editor Kees Boonman; […]” The description of the broadcast on the archive page in De Schatkamer, up to and including the first of its twelve items. The characters ‘-*’ are formatting remnants in the archive description and are left as they stand. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“20 uur Journaal, gepresenteerd door Pia Dijkstra, met de volgende onderwerpen: minister De Grave ontslaat top militaire inlichtingendienst MID: verklaring van De Grave; -*kruisgesprek met politiek redacteur Kees Boonman; […]”

Why this document matters

In June 1999 Giltay lodged a complaint of slander and libel against Barbara Overduyn with Chief Public Prosecutor Van Gend. Two weeks later the Minister of Defence removed the head of the Military Intelligence Service, H.J. Vandeweijer, and his deputy, R. Wielinga, from their posts, and this bulletin opens with De Grave’s own statement on that decision. It shows how the removal was presented to the public on the day itself.
