National Ombudsman to Giltay, with Giltay’s letter of 13 March 2018, 10 April 2018

The passage

“We have come to the conclusion that further correspondence on these subjects will not lead to a different opinion. With this letter we wish to bring the discussion between you and us on these subjects to an end. Further letters from you on the same matter will merely be noted. That means that these documents will be read and archived, but that you will receive no reply to them.” Page 17, the middle paragraph of the letter. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Wij komen tot de conclusie dat verder corresponderen over deze onderwerpen niet tot een ander oordeel zal leiden. De discussie tussen u en ons over deze onderwerpen willen wij met deze brief beëindigen. Verdere brieven van u over dezelfde kwestie zullen voor kennisgeving worden aangenomen. Dat betekent dat deze stukken wel worden gelezen en gearchiveerd, maar dat u daarop geen reactie ontvangt.” The Dutch phrase ‘voor kennisgeving aangenomen’ is an administrative formula: a document is registered as received and then filed without an answer.

Why this document matters

On page 17 of the PDF the National Ombudsman wrote to Giltay on 10 April 2018 that Giltay’s letter of 13 March 2018 gave him no reason “to revisit the position we took earlier”. The Ombudsman declined to respond grievance by grievance, brought the discussion to an end, and announced that further letters would be read and archived but not answered. For the new complaints about the Ministry of Defence, the letter refers Giltay to the Minister of Defence first, because the Ombudsman takes up no complaint before the body complained about has had the chance to respond.

The same PDF opens with the letter of 13 March 2018, in which Giltay sets out nine grievances, attaches nineteen exhibits and asks the Ombudsman to put the mistake right, to withdraw the disputed report and to explain that step publicly. The essay states that the ministerial characterisation of Giltay remains online today, in the National Ombudsman’s public report of 1999, and that in 2018, after the judicial rehabilitation, the institution refused to address his grievances on the merits.
