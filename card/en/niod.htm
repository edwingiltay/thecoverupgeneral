Catalogue entry of the NIOD Library, 2015

The passage

“1 result in NIOD Biblio­theek · De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal : overheids­spionage op de bedrijfs­vloer · Authors: Edwin Giltay 1970- (Author) · Print book 2014 · Amsterdam : SpeakEasy Publisher & Agency, [2014] · Summary: Casema employee reveals spying by the military in­tel­li­gence service aimed at burying evidence of war crimes in the former Yu­go­sla­via. NL-ZmNBD.” Page 1, the result line and the catalogue entry, field by field: number of results, title, author, format and year, place of publication and publisher, and the summary. The book’s title is left in Dutch; its subtitle means ‘government espionage on the shop floor’. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“1 resultaat in NIOD Biblio­theek · De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal : overheids­spionage op de bedrijfs­vloer · Auteurs: Edwin Giltay 1970- (Auteur) · Gedrukt boek 2014 · Amsterdam : SpeakEasy Publisher & Agency, [2014] · Overzicht: Casema-mede­werker onthult spionage­activiteiten van de militaire inlichtingen­dienst die erop gericht waren bewijzen van oorlogs­misdaden in voormalig Joegoslavië in de doofpot te stoppen. NL-ZmNBD.” The fields appear on separate lines in the catalogue entry and are joined here.

Why this document matters

The library catalogue of the NIOD Institute for War, Holocaust and Genocide Studies in Amsterdam returns one result, the printed 2014 edition, whose summary ends with the code NL-ZmNBD. That code shows that the summary comes from NBD Biblion in Zoetermeer, the organisation that writes descriptions of new books for Dutch libraries. The essay makes the point that the book now sits in the collections that preserve the history it describes, including that of the NIOD.
