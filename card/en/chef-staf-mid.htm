In­ter­nal in­ves­ti­ga­tion for the MID Chief of Staff, 12 February 1999

The essay cites this report four times: for the spelling ‘Overduin’, which the author took from it, for Overduyn’s claim that Giltay was “completely insane”, for the interview from which the ministerial position emerged, and for her statement that she had advised Giltay to write an unsolicited letter of application to the MID. The Voices section also quotes what she said about his dismissal. The three statements are set out below.

What Overduyn said about his dismissal

“After those three days Giltay is said to have been dismissed for ‘irritating and maladjusted behaviour’.” Page 1, the first bullet of Overduyn’s statement, in the officer’s indirect speech. In the original, ‘wegen’ is a typing error for ‘wegens’.

“Na deze drie dagen zou GILTAY zijn ontslagen wegen “irritant en onaangepast gedrag”.” Original wording

What Overduyn said about applying to the MID

“In addition, she advised him to write an unsolicited letter of application to the MID.” Page 1, the closing sentence of the fourth bullet of Overduyn’s statement, in the officer’s indirect speech. The Dutch sentence has no subject of its own: the subject is the word ‘Zij’ (‘She’) with which the bullet opens, and the English supplies it. An ‘open sollicitatiebrief’ is a letter of application that does not respond to an advertised vacancy.

“Hem daarnaast heeft geadviseerd een open sollicitatiebrief te schrijven aan de MID.” Original wording

What Overduyn said about his state of mind

“She shared the view of her Casema colleagues that Giltay was ‘completely insane’.” Page 1, one of the later bullets of the same statement, again in indirect speech; the sentence runs on from the opening line ‘OVERDUIN verklaarde:’. This is the sentence that gave rise to the ministerial position that Giltay was “completely insane”. The report writes the names in capitals and misspells hers as ‘OVERDUIN’ instead of Overduyn.

“Zij de mening van haar CASEMA collegae deelde dat GILTAY “volledig gestoord” was.” Original wording

Why this document matters

This note of 12 February 1999 is the report of the investigation the MID opened into itself, written on the instruction of the service’s Chief of Staff, R. Wielinga. In it Overduyn states that Giltay, placed at Casema as a Randstad temp, had supposedly been dismissed for “irritating and maladjusted behaviour”, and that she shared her Casema colleagues’ view that he was “completely insane”. She also states that she had advised him “to write an unsolicited letter of application to the MID”. The service’s own report thus records that at Casema she recommended that Giltay apply to the MID.

The investigation followed Giltay’s complaint to the National Ombudsman. The Ministry of Defence then enclosed the report with its letter of 2 March 1999 to the Ombudsman, and the Minister thereby adopted Overduyn’s account as his own position.
