NIOD report, passage on the briefing by Major De Ruyter, 2002

The passage

“Important for the picture that Dutchbat III formed of the situation on the ground was the briefing given by the military security officer Major De Ruyter. According to Warrant Officer W. Dijkema, during the training [De Ruyter] taught the classes that the population of the enclave consisted of ‘nothing but scum’. In the Balkans things were quite different from at home, and ‘the Balkan man’ was literally cast as ‘the Other’.” Page 1489, the paragraph beginning ‘Belangrijk voor de beeldvorming’, in Part II, chapter 8, section 3, on stereotypes and the training. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Belangrijk voor de beeldvorming over de situatie ter plaatse bij Dutchbat III is de voorlichting van de militaire veiligheidsfunctionaris majoor De Ruyter geweest. Volgens adjudant W. Dijkema leerde deze de klassen tijdens de opleiding dat de bevolking in de enclave uit ‘louter tuig’ bestond. Op de Balkan was het heel anders dan thuis en ‘de Balkan-mens’ werd letterlijk neergezet als ‘de Ander’.” The report is set in justified lines and words are broken at the line ends; the breaks inside ‘voor-lichting’ and ‘adju-dant’ have been closed up here. The Dutch ‘adjudant’ is a warrant-officer rank, and ‘de Balkan-mens’, rendered above as ‘the Balkan man’, is the report’s own name for the stereotype it is describing.

Why this document matters

The NIOD report, the official government-commissioned inquiry into the fall of Srebrenica, records, on the word of Warrant Officer W. Dijkema, that the military security officer Major De Ruyter taught the soldiers deployed as Dutchbat III, during their training, that the population of the enclave consisted of “nothing but scum”. According to the essay, this counter-intelligence major took receipt of the roll of film.

The report of the internal MID investigation of 12 February 1999, signed by Major R.F.J.H. de Ruijter, records the interview with Overduyn on 11 February 1999 in which she shared the view of her Casema colleagues that Giltay was “completely insane”. National Ombudsman report 1999/507 includes that report in the Minister’s position. That it was the same Major De Ruyter who interviewed her is stated in the book (pp. 56 and 64).
