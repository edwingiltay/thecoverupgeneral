Roelie Post on Twitter, 10 Au­gust 2021

The passage

“That’s nice. By the way, am now reading your book, de doofpot generaal. Opens up a world for me too!” The whole of the post, below the line marking it as a reply to @edwingiltay. Translated from the Dutch as the author himself translates it in note 408 of the book; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“Dat is mooi. Ben trouwens nu jouw boek, de doofpot generaal, aan het lezen. Gaat ook een wereld voor me open!” Roelie Post writes the title in lower case and as two words, ‘de doofpot generaal’. The second sentence carries no subject and the third lacks ‘er’ (there), in the clipped style of the platform. In the post the last sentence stands on a line of its own; it is run on here, as in note 408 of the book. The raw text opens with the address ‘@edwingiltay’, but X sets that above the post as the line naming whom she is answering, so it is not part of the wording quoted here.

Why this document matters

Roelie Post is a whistleblower of another kind, an official at the European Commission who worked on combating child trafficking. The post is her reply to Giltay, who had ordered her book that morning; in it she tells him that she is now reading his book and that it opens up a world for her too. In a telephone conversation on 8 December 2021 she approved the fuller wording that the Voices section quotes, in which she asks who her own cover-up general is, and whether she should actually say ‘cover-up secretary-general’.
