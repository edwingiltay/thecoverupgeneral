Exhibit · Letter

Police district chief to Giltay, 26 March 2000

The passage

“Like [redacted], I am of the opinion that the detectives should have recorded your criminal complaint during your first visits to Duinstraat police station. I regret that this did not happen and therefore consider your grievance well founded on this point. I also regret that you were left with the impression that you were not taken seriously by the two detectives, partly because of an incident during your conversation with detective [redacted]. On this point too I consider your grievance well founded. For both points deemed well founded above, I offer my apologies.” Page 1, the paragraph in which the grievance is upheld. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. The square brackets mark two names that are blacked out in the document itself.

Original wording

“Met […] ben ik van mening dat de rechercheurs tijdens uw eerste bezoeken aan bureau Duinstraat uw aangifte hadden moeten opnemen. Ik betreur het feit dat dit niet is gebeurd en acht uw klacht op dit punt dan ook gegrond. Tevens betreur ik het feit dat bij u de indruk is ontstaan dat u niet serieus bent genomen door de beide rechercheurs, mede door een incident tijdens uw gesprek met rechercheur […]. Ook op dit punt acht ik uw klacht gegrond. Voor beide hierboven gegrond geachte punten bied ik mijn verontschuldiging aan.” The two bracketed ellipses stand for two names that are blacked out in the document. The letterhead spells the district chief’s name as ‘N.. Laagland’, the signature as ‘N. Laagland’.

Why this document matters

When Giltay, on the advice of lawyer Gerard Spong, tried to file a criminal complaint in July 1999, the police in The Hague refused to record it, according to the book (p. 66). In this letter the district chief accepts that the detectives should have recorded the criminal complaint, and that Giltay was left with the impression that he had not been taken seriously. He upholds the grievance on both points and offers his apologies. Only after this, in June 2000, did The Hague police record the complaint of slander and libel.
