Bram van Ojik to Giltay, email, 12 Feb­ru­ary 2015

The passage

“I still remember our meeting at the IJsbreker in Amsterdam well. Your book makes the need for robust external oversight of intelligence and security services all too clear.” The two sentences after the salutation. The paragraph beneath them is blacked out in the printout. The email spells the name “IJsbreker”; the correct spelling is Ysbreker. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Ik kan mij onze ontmoeting in de Amsterdamse IJsbreker nog goed herinneren. Je boek maakt de noodzaak van stevige externe controle op inlichtingen- en veiligheidsdiensten maar al te duidelijk.”

Why this document matters

Bram van Ojik, then leader of the GroenLinks group in the House of Representatives, wrote to Giltay after the two had met at the Ysbreker café-restaurant in Amsterdam on 21 November 2014, saying that the book makes the need for robust external oversight of the intelligence and security services all too clear. The Voices section quotes the second sentence of this email. The use of the quotation was approved by telephone on 17 February 2015 by Willemijn Boss, Van Ojik’s personal assistant.
