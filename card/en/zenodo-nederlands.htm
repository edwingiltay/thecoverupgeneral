Zenodo record for the Dutch edition, deposited 1 July 2026

The passage

“This third edition is distributed worldwide as a free PDF, as a protest against censorship. Documentation, source documents and 72 verified quotations are available at https://www.dedoofpotgeneraal.nl.” The fourth paragraph of the Description field on the record page, zenodo.org/records/21116205. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Deze derde druk wordt als PDF gratis wereldwijd verspreid, als protest tegen censuur. Documentatie, brondocumenten en 72 geverifieerde citaten zijn beschikbaar op https://www.dedoofpotgeneraal.nl.” The Description field also carries an English abstract by the author; the passage above is translated from the Dutch text and is not taken from that abstract. The number of verified quotations is the figure as it stood when the record was deposited in July 2026.

Why this document matters

This Zenodo record holds the Dutch edition under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21116205: one file of 304 pages, the third, revised edition with an afterword by Hans Laroes, released under a Creative Commons Attribution licence. A DOI is a permanent reference that keeps working even if the web address changes. The essay states that in July 2026 both editions were deposited in the archive of CERN in Geneva, so that they remain permanently findable and citable. Under ‘Related works’ the record refers to the DOI of the English edition, the second identifier the essay names.
