The Fearless Ber­di­che­ver: essay for the beit din, 12 August 2025

The passage

“My life path was shaped by my role as a witness to a Dutch cover-up surrounding the Srebrenica genocide, as detailed in my book The Cover-Up General (Giltay, 2014) and affirmed by a Dutch court ruling (ECLI:NL:GHDHA:2016:870). Years of struggle and legal proceedings brought me closer to Rebbe Levi Yitzchak, who, like me, fought against injustice with remarkable impact. His chutzpah, gevurah, and kedusha deeply inspire me.” Page 3, from the letter to the beit din that opens the essay. The document is in English.

Why this document matters

The author biography on this site records that Giltay met years of pressure by deepening his spiritual life, and that he laid before the rabbinical court, among other things, an essay on the commandment to pursue justice. This is that essay, a reflection on the eighteenth-century Rebbe Levi Yitzchak of Berdichev, who stood up for his community before a prince and before heaven itself. In the covering letter Giltay ties that example to his own path. On the advice of his rabbi he also describes in the essay his role as a witness to a Dutch cover-up.
