Giltay to the Inter­na­tion­al Criminal Court, 26 and 31 March 2026

The passage

“On 25 March 2026, the author addressed Judge Solomy Balungi Bossa of the International Criminal Court at a public debate in Amare, The Hague (PDF), in sight of the Ministry of Defence. In the context of Srebrenica and this book, he asked what she thought of those who cover up evidence of war crimes. Her answer was unambiguous: ‘They should be brought to justice.’” Page 2, from the covering note. It is the paragraph Giltay put to the Court in his email of 26 March 2026 (page 1).

Why this document matters

At the public debate in Amare, The Hague, within sight of the Ministry of Defence, Giltay asked the Ugandan judge Solomy Balungi Bossa of the International Criminal Court what she thought of those who cover up evidence of war crimes. She is one of the five judges of the Appeals Chamber, the Court’s highest judicial chamber. According to the paragraph he put to the Court, she answered that those who do so should be brought to justice. Before publishing that exchange he emailed the Court twice, on 26 and 31 March 2026, asking it to put the paragraph to the judge and to invite her comments or objections. As of 14 April 2026, the document records, no response had been received from Judge Bossa or from the Court.
