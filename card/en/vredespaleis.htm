Peace Palace Library catalogue entry, 23 March 2026

The passage

“Casema employee reveals espionage activities by the military intelligence service which were aimed at covering up evidence of war crimes in the former Yugoslavia. […] Available / Peace Palace Library / Closed Stacks (for loan) / 500 G 13” The field labelled ‘Overzicht’ (Summary) in the catalogue record and, under ‘Opties voor toegang’ (Access options), the availability entry, page 1. The slashes mark line breaks. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. ‘Closed Stacks (for loan)’ is in English in the record.

Original wording

“Casema-medewerker onthult spionageactiviteiten van de militaire inlichtingendienst die erop gericht waren bewijzen van oorlogsmisdaden in voormalig Joegoslavië in de doofpot te stoppen. […] Beschikbaar / Peace Palace Library / Closed Stacks (for loan) / 500 G 13” The record closes the summary with the code ‘NL-ZmNBD’, which falls outside the quotation.

Why this document matters

The Peace Palace Library in The Hague holds the 2014 edition of De doofpotgeneraal, available for loan from its closed stacks and indexed under the subject headings war crimes, espionage and intelligence agencies. The essay notes that the book now sits in the collections that preserve the history it describes.
