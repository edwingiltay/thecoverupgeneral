Charlef Brantz, Srebrenica: De Schuldigen, passage on the film roll, 10 July 2020

The passage

“6. first week of August 1995
His outburst after my remarks during one of the telephone interviews with NOVA, in which I had voiced my doubts about the course of action, as described by the KL, that was said to have led to the ‘unwitting’ destruction of the Rutten film roll. I could not imagine that a film roll regarded as important by the Defence Staff and the KL would have been handled so carelessly that it led to the destruction of the photographic material. During the interview I used the words: ‘an organisation that takes itself seriously, such as the KL, cannot afford to handle that material in such a careless manner.’ Van Baal telephoned me and stated that my words had caused a great deal of commotion at the Ministry.” Page 110, item 6 in a numbered series; the first line is the heading of that item. ‘His’ in the first sentence refers to Van Baal, who is named at the end of the passage; KL stands for Koninklijke Landmacht, the Royal Netherlands Army. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“6. eerste week van augustus 1995
Zijn uitbarsting na mijn uitspraken tijdens een van de telefonische interviews met NOVA waarin ik mijn twijfels geuit had over de door de KL weergegeven handelwijze die tot de ‘onbewuste’ vernietiging van het Rutten filmrolletje zou hebben geleid. Ik kon me niet voorstellen dat een filmrolletje dat door Defensiestaf en KL als belangrijk werd beschouwd op een zo’n nonchalante manier zou zijn behandeld dat het tot een vernietiging van het fotomateriaal leidde. Ik gebruikte tijdens het interview de tekst: “een zichzelf serieus nemende organisatie als de KL kan het zich niet veroorloven op een dergelijke nonchalante manier met dat materiaal om te gaan.” Van Baal belde me op en verklaarde dat mijn woorden tot veel commotie op het Ministerie hadden geleid.” Line breaks in the scan have been closed up. The words ‘op een zo’n’ are as printed in the book.

Why this document matters

In July 1995 Colonel Charlef Brantz was chief of staff and acting commander of UN Sector North East in Tuzla, to which Dutchbat in Srebrenica was subordinate. In his 2020 book Srebrenica: De Schuldigen (‘Srebrenica: The Guilty’) he writes that in the first week of August 1995, in a telephone interview with the Dutch current affairs programme NOVA, he had voiced his doubts about the course of action that, according to the Royal Netherlands Army, had led to the ‘unwitting’ destruction of Rutten’s film roll. He could not imagine that a film roll regarded as important by the Defence Staff and the army had been handled so carelessly. According to Brantz, he was then telephoned and told that his words had caused a great deal of commotion at the ministry. The passage is his own rendering, written in 2020, of what he said in 1995; the essay therefore states that, by his own account, he voiced his doubts, and sets them beside the account that General Hans Couzy, who commanded the Dutch army in 1995, gave in 1998.
