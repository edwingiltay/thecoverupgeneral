Speaker Khadija Arib to Giltay, 27 June 2017

The passage

“Thank you very much for your letter of 1 June, in which you share your story. Given my neutral position as Speaker of the House of Representatives, I cannot respond to its substance. I am therefore forwarding your letter to the Standing Committee on Defence for consideration.” Page 1, the body of the letter, between the salutation and the closing. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Hartelijk dank voor uw brief van 1 juni jl., waarin u uw verhaal deelt. Gezien mijn neutrale positie als Voorzitter van de Tweede Kamer kan ik daar niet inhoudelijk op ingaan. Ik stuur u brief dan ook ter behandeling door aan de Vaste Commissie voor Defensie.” Line breaks in the scan have been closed up. The last sentence reads ‘Ik stuur u brief’ rather than ‘uw brief’. That slip is in the letter itself, as the scan shows, and is reproduced here; the translation renders it as intended.

Why this document matters

In this letter of 27 June 2017 the Speaker of the House, Khadija Arib, tells Giltay that she is forwarding his letter of 1 June 2017 to the Standing Committee on Defence for consideration, because her neutral position keeps her from commenting on its substance herself. The essay states that the Standing Committee on Defence’s 2017 request for a ministerial answer had been preceded by this step on the Speaker’s part. That committee took the matter up and asked the Minister of Defence for a response.
