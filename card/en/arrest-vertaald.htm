Court of Ap­peal of The Hague, judgment of 12 April 2016, un­of­fi­cial Eng­lish trans­la­tion

The passage

“On the one hand, (an affirmation of) the granting of the ban sought would result in a serious restriction of the freedom of expression. At least part of the book, after all, concerns matters that can be the subject of a debate of public interest. Thus Giltay states in his book, among other things: i) that the Ministry of Defence systematically rejects persons applying to the marine corps on the ground of homosexuality; ii) that the roll of film with photographs that a soldier of Dutchbat took in Srebrenica was not destroyed by accident, but was deliberately withheld by the MID and is still in the MID’s archives; iii) that Overduyn was placed under observation because she expressed herself critically within the MID about the withholding of the roll of film, and was subsequently dismissed. A restriction of Giltay’s freedom to raise these matters and to express his opinion on them would affect the fundamental freedom of expression at its core.” Page 5, para. 4.5 of the judgment, quoted from the unofficial English translation itself. Only the Dutch original is authoritative. The operative part, para. 5.1.1, refuses the ban sought.

Why this document matters

This is the judgment that lifted the ban on The Cover-up General (De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal), made accessible to those who do not read Dutch. In para. 4.5 the Court of Appeal sets out in its own words three of the book’s claims. The core claim is that the roll of film with the photographs a Dutchbat soldier took in Srebrenica was not destroyed by accident but was deliberately withheld by the MID and is still in the MID’s archives. The court did not establish that this is true, but it did rule that such matters can be the subject of a debate of public interest and that restricting Giltay’s freedom to raise them would affect the fundamental freedom of expression at its core. Only the Dutch original is authoritative, and this translation carries no official status.
