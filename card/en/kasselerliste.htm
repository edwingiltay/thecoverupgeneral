Why this document matters

Die Kasseler Liste, a database of censored books, lists The Cover-up General, with the Netherlands as the country of the ban, 2015 as the year of the ban and 2016 as the year it was lifted, and cites the 2016 judgment of the Court of Appeal of The Hague as its source. The essay makes the point that the censorship carried the story around the world, and this entry is one trace of that.
