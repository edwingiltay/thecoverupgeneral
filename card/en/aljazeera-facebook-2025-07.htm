Al Ja­zeera Doc­u­men­tary photo story on Facebook, 11 July 2025

The passage

“The Srebrenica massacre, which killed some 8,000 Muslims in Bosnia, still causes controversy today, but a Dutch book that was banned 10 years ago sheds light on other dimensions of the case. What is its story?” The caption of the post, shown above the images, in full apart from the two hashtags that follow it. Trans­lat­ed from the Arabic; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“ما تزال مجزرة سربرنيتسا التي قتلت نحو 8 آلاف مسلم في البوسنة تثير الجدل حتى الآن، لكن كتابا هولنديا حُظر قبل 10 سنوات يروي أبعادا أخرى في القضية. فما قصته؟” The caption is written in Arabic. It spells Srebrenica as سربرنيتسا. The two hashtags that close the caption are left out of the quotation.

Why this document matters

On 11 July 2025, thirty years after the fall of Srebrenica, Al Jazeera Documentary also posted on Facebook the photo story that had appeared on Instagram in April, for its Arabic-speaking audience.
