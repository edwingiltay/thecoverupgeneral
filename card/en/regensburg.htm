Why this document matters

The Regensburger Katalog lists one copy of the English edition in the stacks of the Leibniz Institute for East and Southeast European Studies, under shelf mark W 01/2026.2300, and makes it available to order. Page 2 shows the full catalogue record, in which the library also names the website accompanying the book and notes that the site can be consulted in five languages. The essay counts the institute among the collections that preserve the history the book describes.
