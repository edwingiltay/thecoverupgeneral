DAS on behalf of Bar­ba­ra Over­duyn to publisher SpeakEasy, cease-and-desist letter, 24 July 2015

The passage

“I should like to hear from you within 10 days of the date of this letter that you are prepared to accommodate my client by agreeing to / cooperating in: - A rectification in a national daily newspaper - full indemnification of my client - a ban on further publication (both reprint and digital) - a recall of the copies already distributed If you do not respond, do not respond in time or do not respond adequately, I am instructed to bring proceedings against you and Mr Giltay in order to bring all this about.” Page 2, last paragraph, and page 3, first paragraph. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Graag verneem ik binnen 10 dagen na dagtekening dezes van u dat u bereid bent om cliënte tegemoet te komen door in te stemmen met / mee te werken aan: - Een rectificatie in een landelijk dagblad - volledig schadeloos van cliënte - een verbod op verdere publicatie (zowel herdruk en digitaal) - een terugroepactie van de reeds gedistribueerde exemplaren Indien u niet, niet tijdig of niet adequaat reageert dan heb ik de opdracht u en de heer Giltay in rechte te betrekken om een en ander te bewerkstelligen.” The line breaks have been closed up here; ‘volledig schadeloos van cliënte’ is how the letter itself puts it.

Why this document matters

On 24 July 2015 the legal expenses insurer DAS wrote, on behalf of Barbara Overduyn, to the publisher of The Cover-up General that the book damaged her honour and good name. The letter lists fourteen points that she says are incorrect, among them the claim that she knows that the Srebrenica film roll was not ruined but is held by the MID. It demands a rectification, compensation, a ban on further publication and a recall; failing a satisfactory answer, publisher and author will be taken to court. The letter was written seventeen days after the Mothers of Srebrenica cited the book in their statement of appeal of 7 July 2015. The biography refers to it as the cease-and-desist letter that led in December 2015 to the ban on both publication and speech.
