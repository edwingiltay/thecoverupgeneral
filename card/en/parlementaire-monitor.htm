Harry van Bommel, blog post, 15 April 2016

The passage

“At the moment no copies of the book are available, but the second edition is in preparation. Just as well, because a book ban belongs to another era. I have read the book and can recommend it to everyone. It is very thrilling.” Closing sentences of the post. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Momenteel zijn er geen exemplaren van het boek verkrijgbaar, maar de tweede druk van het boek is in voorbereiding. Gelukkig maar, want een boekverbod is niet van deze tijd. Ik heb het boek gelezen en kan het iedereen aanraden. Het is erg spannend.”

Why this document matters

Writing days after the Court of Appeal of The Hague quashed the distribution ban imposed the previous year, a sitting member of the House of Representatives said publicly why he thought a book ban was wrong. Van Bommel wrote that such a ban belongs to another era, that he had read the book himself and that he recommended it to everyone. He also recorded that no copies were to be had at that moment, with a second edition in preparation.
