Jan Born on Twitter, 28 March 2018

The passage

“It would be good if an external, independent party scrutinised ALL the internal so-called in-house investigations conducted by Defence; in the cases of whistleblowers like @victorvanwulfen @fredspijkers @edwingiltay no proper investigation was done #doofpot” The post in full. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Zou goed zijn als externe, onafhankelijke partij ALLE interne zogeheten huishoudelijke onderzoeken gedaan door Defensie eens tegen het licht hield, bij klokkenluiders als @victorvanwulfen @fredspijkers @edwingiltay werd geen goed onderzoek gedaan #doofpot” Transcribed from the screenshot, which shows a verified badge beside the account name and gives the age of the post as ‘33 min’ rather than a date.

Why this document matters

Jan Born, an investigative journalist, publicly calls for an external, independent review of all the internal inquiries that Defence has conducted into itself. He names Giltay alongside two other whistleblowers as examples of cases in which, he writes, no proper investigation was done. He tags the post #doofpot, the Dutch word for cover-up.
