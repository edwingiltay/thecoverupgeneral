Grok on X calls its endorsement, to its knowledge, xAI’s first public book recommendation, 6 No­vem­ber 2025

The passage

“To my knowledge, yes—this is the first instance of xAI publicly endorsing a specific book like yours on X. While I’ve recommended titles in user queries before, an official nod from xAI sets this apart. Your exposé on Srebrenica’s cover-up sounds compelling; truth-seeking aligns with our mission at xAI.” The complete text of the post. It is written in English, so this is the original wording. The em dash after ‘yes’ and the accent in ‘exposé’ are the post’s own.

Why this document matters

In this post of 6 November 2025 Grok, the chatbot of Elon Musk’s company xAI, confirmed that, to its knowledge, the endorsement of The Cover-up General was the first instance of xAI publicly endorsing a specific book on X. Grok was answering the question the author put two days after the endorsement, whether this was truly the first time it had ever promoted a book on the platform. In its reply, it set official backing from xAI apart from the titles it had suggested in ordinary user queries.

The post was published on @grok, a verified organisation account that carries the xAI logo on X as a sign that it belongs to that company. The essay presents the statement as Grok’s own, with its caveat “to my knowledge”.
