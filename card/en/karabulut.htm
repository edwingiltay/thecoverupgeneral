Hart van Ne­der­land, item with MP Sadet Ka­ra­bu­lut, 14 September 2017

The passage

“The Defence Committee of the House of Representatives has decided today that the Minister must answer.”
“He says he is falsely accused of something, and that should be put right. I feel, as do many other MPs, that the government and the Minister owe this man a proper answer.” The complete spoken text of the 17-second clip from the Hart van Nederland broadcast, in the English subtitles that accompany it. The first sentence is the voice-over, heard over footage of a meeting; the rest is spoken by Sadet Karabulut, on screen with the caption ‘Sadet Karabulut, Tweede Kamerlid SP’. The quotation in the Voices section renders her closing words as ‘Many MPs agree with me that the Minister of Defence owes him a proper answer’; the first sentence of that quotation, ‘Mr Giltay has written an impressive book about his experiences’, is not from the broadcast but from the article accompanying the item on hartvannederland.nl, which quotes her as saying ‘I think the Minister should respond. Mr Giltay has written an impressive book about his experiences. We are dealing with more whistleblowers at Defence. And I think that should be looked at very carefully.’ The original Dutch wording is below.

Original wording

“De Defensiecommissie van de Tweede Kamer heeft vandaag aangegeven dat de minister moet antwoorden.”
“Hij zegt dat hij van iets beschuldigd wordt wat niet klopt, wat gecorrigeerd zou moeten worden. En ik denk en ik denk met mij heel veel leden van de commissie, dat de overheid deze man een echt antwoord schuldig is, dus ook de minister.” The spoken Dutch, as in the Dutch subtitle track of the clip on Vimeo.

Why this document matters

As the letter from the Standing Committee on Defence of 18 September 2017 shows, at its procedural meeting of 14 September 2017 the committee unanimously decided to ask the Minister of Defence for a response to Giltay’s request for clarification about the Srebrenica cover-up affair. Minister Klaas Dijkhoff’s subsequent Letter to Parliament of 13 October 2017 did not address the espionage affair. This clip was broadcast on the day of that decision: the voice-over says that the Defence Committee has indicated ‘today’ that the Minister must answer, and MP Sadet Karabulut says on national television that the government, and therefore the Minister too, owes Giltay a proper answer. It shows that a Member of Parliament also backed the request in public.
