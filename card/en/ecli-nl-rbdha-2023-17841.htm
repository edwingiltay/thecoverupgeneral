District Court of The Hague, judgment of 18 July 2023 (ECLI:­NL:­RBDHA:­2023:­17841)

The Voices section quotes two sentences: the first appears in para. 4.8 of the judgment, the second in the summary on rechtspraak.nl. Both are set out below, with the judge’s conclusion between them.

Para. 4.8 · The decision is manifestly unlawful

“4.8. Now that it is established that the first option referred to under 4.6 above does not arise, and that the respondent has not refused the applicant’s representative under Article 2:2 of the General Administrative Law Act, there is no obstacle whatsoever to the applicant’s representative submitting a Woo request on his behalf, nor is there any ground for then declining to consider that request by reference to other cases in which that representative is accused of abuse of rights. The contested decision is thus manifestly unlawful. The lack of urgency is therefore not held against the applicant in this case.” Para. 4.8, the last before the heading ‘Conclusie en gevolgen’. ‘Beletstel’ in the original is a slip for ‘beletsel’ (obstacle) and has been left as it stands. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

“4.8. Nu vaststaat dat de hiervoor onder 4.6 genoemde eerste optie niet aan de orde is en de gemachtigde van verzoeker niet geweigerd is door verweerder op grond van artikel 2:2 van de Awb, bestaat geen enkel beletstel voor de gemachtigde van verzoeker om namens hem een Woo-verzoek in te dienen en bestaat evenmin een grond om dat verzoek vervolgens buiten behandeling te stellen met verwijzing naar andere zaken waarin die gemachtigde misbruik van recht wordt verweten. Daarmee is er sprake van een evidente onrechtmatigheid van het bestreden besluit. Het gebrek aan spoedeisend belang wordt verzoeker daarom in dit geval niet tegengeworpen.” Original wording

Paras 5.1 and 5.2 · Conclusion and consequences

“5.1. Since the contested decision is manifestly unlawful and further investigation cannot contribute to the assessment of the case, the preliminary relief judge sees reason to give judgment at once in the main action, applying Article 8:86, first paragraph, of the General Administrative Law Act. 5.2. The preliminary relief judge will declare the appeal well founded and quash the contested decision. This means that the respondent [the National Ombudsman] will still have to decide on the applicant’s request, having regard to this judgment.” Page 6 of the judgment, paras 5.1 and 5.2, under the heading ‘Conclusie en gevolgen’ (Conclusion and consequences). Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. ‘Verweerder’, the respondent, is the National Ombudsman; the words in square brackets have been added here. The operative part on page 7 lists the decisions in four lines: the appeal is declared well founded, the contested decision is quashed, a fresh decision on the Woo request is ordered, and the request for an interim measure is refused. The version published on deeplink.rechtspraak.nl/uitspraak?id=ECLI:NL:RBDHA:2023:17841 anonymises the parties: the applicant appears as [eiser] and his representative as [gemachtigde]. The copy on this site names the applicant, E.F. Giltay of The Hague. Para. 5.1 reads ‘onmiddellijk uitspraak doen’ where Dutch grammar requires ‘onmiddellijk uitspraak te doen’; the slip is in the original and has been left as it stands.

“5.1. Nu het bestreden besluit evident onrechtmatig is en nader onderzoek niet kan bijdragen aan de beoordeling van de zaak, ziet de voorzieningenrechter reden om met toepassing van artikel 8:86, eerste lid, van de Awb onmiddellijk uitspraak doen in de hoofdzaak. 5.2. De voorzieningenrechter zal het beroep gegrond verklaren en het bestreden besluit vernietigen. Dit betekent dat verweerder alsnog zal moeten beslissen op het verzoek van verzoeker met inachtneming van deze uitspraak.” Original wording

Summary on rechtspraak.nl

“The contested decision is thus manifestly unlawful. The respondent must still issue a decision on the Woo request.” The last two sentences of the summary that rechtspraak.nl places above the published judgment. That summary is written by rechtspraak.nl and is not part of the judgment itself; the first sentence appears word for word in para. 4.8, the second sums up para. 5.2. ‘Verweerder’, the respondent, is the National Ombudsman. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

“Daarmee is er sprake van een evidente onrechtmatigheid van het bestreden besluit. Verweerder zal alsnog op het Woo-verzoek moeten beslissen.” Original wording

Why this document matters

In this judgment of 18 July 2023, the preliminary relief judge held that the National Ombudsman could not decline to consider Giltay’s request of 13 April 2023 under the Open Government Act (Woo) merely because abuse of rights had been assumed on the part of the applicant’s authorised representative, all the more so because the Ombudsman did not accuse Giltay himself of anything of the kind. The contested decision was therefore manifestly unlawful, the appeal was declared well founded, and the institution was ordered to take a fresh decision on the request. The essay states that the National Ombudsman broke off the correspondence and then blocked this request for government documents. A second judgment, ECLI:NL:RBDHA:2023:20409, later attached penalty payments to the failure to decide on another Woo request of 1 September 2023.
