EenVandaag, item on De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal and the film roll, 5 De­cem­ber 2014

The passage

“Edwin Giltay is an ordinary citizen, yet he became tangentially involved in the scandal. It would not let go of him, and he wrote the book de Doofpotgeneraal about it.” The body of the article, below the introduction. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“Edwin Giltay is een gewone burger, maar raakte toch zijdelings bij het schandaal betrokken. Dat liet hem niet meer los en hij schreef er het boek de Doofpotgeneraal over.” The article sets the book title in italics and gives it in Dutch as “de Doofpotgeneraal”, with the definite article in lower case.

Why this document matters

On 5 December 2014 Een­Van­daag, the news and current affairs programme of the Dutch public broadcaster, reported that the Public Prosecution Service had to disclose what it knew about the missing film roll from Srebrenica, following a ruling of the court of appeal in a case brought by surviving relatives. Against that background the programme introduced Giltay as an ordinary citizen who nonetheless became tangentially involved in the scandal, could not let it go, and wrote The Cover-up General about it. The essay counts this item as part of the media attention surrounding the book’s publication.
