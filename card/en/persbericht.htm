De Blauwe Tijger press release, 12 Sep­tem­ber 2017

The press release closes with two voices on the psychiatrisation of whistleblowers. The Voices section quotes the second, by Victor van Wulfen.

Freedom of information specialist Roger Vleugels

“The psychiatrisation of whistleblowers is a current problem. WOB specialist Roger Vleugels: ‘In recent years I have seen more and more often that the government uses incorrect and unlawful medical labels to silence staff.’” Page 1, the penultimate paragraph of the press release. The WOB (Wet openbaarheid van bestuur) was the Dutch freedom of information act. The line breaks of the screenshot have been closed up. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

“Het psychiatriseren van klokkenluiders is een actueel probleem. WOB-specialist Roger Vleugels: ‘Ik zie de laatste jaren steeds vaker dat de overheid onjuiste en onrechtmatige medische kwalificaties gebruikt om personeel de mond te snoeren.’” Original wording

F-16 pilot Victor van Wulfen

“F-16 pilot Victor van Wulfen suffered such treatment himself; his medical file was falsified: ‘The wrongful psychiatrisation of those who report abuses is a recurring phenomenon. That is policy, and the Minister of Defence does nothing to stop it.’” Page 1, the closing paragraph of the press release. The line breaks of the screenshot have been closed up. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

“F-16-piloot Victor van Wulfen viel een dergelijke behandeling ten deel – zijn medisch dossier werd vervalst: ‘Het onterecht psychiatriseren van wie misstanden meldt, is een terugkerend verschijnsel. Dat is beleid en de minister van Defensie treedt er niet tegen op.’” Original wording

Why this document matters

With this press release of 12 September 2017 Giltay’s publisher De Blauwe Tijger announced that the Standing Committee on Defence of the House of Representatives would take up his case two days later, noting that a court case had shown that the Minister of Defence’s formal declaration of insanity “rests entirely on quicksand”. On 14 September the committee decided, according to its letter of 18 September 2017, to ask the Minister for a response to Giltay’s letter. The release also quotes a second Defence whistleblower, F-16 pilot Victor van Wulfen, whose medical file the release says was falsified. He calls the wrongful psychiatrisation of those who report abuses a recurring phenomenon and a matter of policy. Those words appear as a quotation in the Voices section.

‘Persbericht. Klokkenluider Edwin Giltay bij de defensiecommissie van de Tweede Kamer’, on whistleblower Edwin Giltay before the Defence committee of the House of Representatives
Contents
That Giltay was wrongly declared ‘disturbed’ by the Minister of Defence years earlier after he raised a scandal within the armed forces; that a court case had since shown, in the release’s words, that this formal declaration of insanity rests entirely on quicksand; that rehabilitation had not followed; and that the Standing Committee on Defence of the House of Representatives would consider his case on the coming Thursday

Edwin Giltay; Roger Vleugels, freedom of information specialist; and Victor van Wulfen, F-16 pilot, whose medical file the release says was falsified
Van Wulfen’s quote
Provided via direct message on Twitter, 10 September 2017, two days before the press release
