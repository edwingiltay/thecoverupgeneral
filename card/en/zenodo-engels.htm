The passage

“This English-language e-book edition (292 pages, edited by Michael Wynne) is distributed free of charge worldwide as a protest against censorship. Its translation and distribution were financed by court-ordered penalty payments following the author’s legal victories over the National Ombudsman of the Netherlands (ECLI:NL:RBDHA:2023:17841 and ECLI:NL:RBDHA:2023:20409). Documentation, source materials and 72 verified quotations are available at https://www.dedoofpotgeneraal.nl.” The fourth paragraph of the Description field on the record page, zenodo.org/records/21115325. The document is in English. The number of verified quotations is the figure as it stood when the book was deposited in July 2026.

Why this document matters

This Zenodo record shows that the English edition was deposited in the archive of CERN in Geneva on 1 July 2026: one file of 292 pages, edited by Michael Wynne, held under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21115325 and released under a Creative Commons Attribution licence. A DOI is a permanent reference that keeps working even if the web address changes. The record states that the translation and the free worldwide distribution were financed by the penalty payments the court imposed after the author had won his cases against the National Ombudsman. The essay states that both editions remain permanently accessible and citable there.
