NBD Bib­lion review of the first edi­tion, 10 April 2015

The passage

“De doofpotgeneraal : overheidsspionage op de bedrijfsvloer is a story, based on Edwin Giltay’s own experiences, about espionage by intelligence services inside a company in the Netherlands. A must-read for anyone who wants a look behind the scenes of government espionage in practice and the dangers it entails for everyone involved. See the heading books, adult non-fiction.” Page 2, the book notice in the NBD Biblion acquisition bulletin, made up of the opening and closing sentences of the review on page 1. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“De doofpotgeneraal : overheidsspionage op de bedrijfsvloer is een op de eigen ervaringen van Edwin Giltay gebaseerd verhaal over spionage door inlichtingendiensten in een bedrijf in Nederland. Een aanrader voor iedereen die een kijkje wil nemen in de keuken van overheidsspionage in de praktijk en de gevaren die dat met zich meebrengt voor alle betrokkenen. Zie de rubriek boeken, non-fictie volwassenen.” The line breaks in the notice have been closed up in the wording above.

Why this document matters

In this review NBD Bib­lion, the review service of the Dutch li­brar­ies, describes De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal as a detailed account of espionage by the intelligence services inside a Dutch company. The reviewer writes that the book reads like a spy novel and recommends it to anyone who wants a look behind the scenes of government espionage. The essay counts this review as part of the critical acclaim the book received in 2015.

In its acquisition bulletin for the libraries, NBD Bib­lion catalogued the book as non-fiction, under the heading ‘boeken, non-fictie volwassenen’ (books, adult non-fiction). Forty-eight libraries then bought the book through NBD Bib­lion. Bib­lion gave Giltay that figure by telephone on 11 May 2015 and he passed it on to his publisher by email the same day. The book (p. 137) records that public libraries across the country added it to their collections.
