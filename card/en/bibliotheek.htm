Royal Li­brary catalogue entry, accessed 17 May 2026

The passage

“A former Ca­se­ma employee reveals espionage activities by the military intelligence service aimed at suppressing evidence of war crimes in the former Yu­go­sla­via.” The summary field of the entry, labelled ‘Overzicht’, page 1. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Een voormalige Casema-medewerker onthult spionageactiviteiten van de militaire inlichtingendienst die erop gericht waren bewijzen van oorlogsmisdaden in voormalig Joegoslavië in de doofpot te stoppen.” The summary closes with the code NL-ZmNBD, left out of the quotation above. Page 2 has the same sentence without the opening words ‘Een voormalige’.

Why this document matters

The Royal Library, the Dutch national library, includes the summary above in its own catalogue. The entry is for the second, updated edition of 2016; the copy sits in the stacks and can be read in the reading room. The second page of the PDF shows the same summary in the public library catalogue at Bibliotheek.nl, which is likewise run by the Royal Library and classifies the book as non-fiction.
