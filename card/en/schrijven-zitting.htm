Schrijven Online, report on the appeal hearing, 26 February 2016

The passage

“As the judge opens the session it immediately becomes clear that Edwin Giltay holds more evidence: 30 exhibits against one. With that interim score hanging in the air, the lawyer Groenendaal begins his address.” Opening of the section ‘Pleidooi appellant’. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“In de opening van de zitting door de rechter wordt meteen duidelijk dat Edwin Giltay meer bewijsmateriaal bezit: 30 bewijsstukken tegenover één. Met deze tussenstand in de lucht begint advocaat Groenendaal aan zijn pleidooi.”

Why this document matters

Schrijven Online, the trade platform of Schrijven Magazine for Dutch authors, was present at the appeal hearing, so this report from the courtroom comes from the writing trade itself. It records the imbalance in the evidence at the opening of the session: 30 exhibits on Giltay’s side against one. It also notes that Thunnissen, counsel for the respondent Barbara Overduyn, cited no exhibits at all in his address. He referred only to a former boss of hers, who was present at the hearing.
