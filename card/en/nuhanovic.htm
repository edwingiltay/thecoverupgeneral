Hasan Nuhanović to Giltay, 27 June 2025

The quotation appears in the author’s request of 24 June 2025, at the foot of the thread; the permission in Hasan Nuhanović’s reply of 27 June above it.

The quotation, in the request of 24 June 2025

“I was wondering if you would be comfortable with me using your statement, ‘I congratulate and wish to thank you for writing and publishing your important book,’ as a quote for my book website at thecoverupgeneral.com.” Page 1, the author’s request of 24 June 2025, quoted at the foot of the thread. The words in the inner quotation marks come from an earlier email by Hasan Nuhanović, as his reply confirms; the request sets them in straight apostrophes. The document is in English.

The permission, 27 June 2025

“I hereby give you my permission to use the quote from my e mail message for the publication of your book.” Page 1, the body of the reply, directly under the salutation. In the message itself, ‘e mail’ is written as two words. The document is in English.

Why this document matters

The Voices section carries a line of congratulation from Hasan Nuhanović, who survived the Srebrenica genocide and wrote Under the UN Flag. This email is the record behind that line. Three days earlier Giltay had asked him whether he could place the statement on his book website with a small photograph beside it and attribute it to Nuhanović, quoting the sentence back word for word. This is the answer: permission given, with good wishes for the book. The words of praise on the site are therefore Nuhanović’s own, used with his written consent.
