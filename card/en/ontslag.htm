Ministry of Defence to Barbara Over­duyn, hon­our­able discharge, 3 November 1998

The passage

“Further to your application of 28 October 1998, I inform you that, pursuant to article 113, paragraphs 1 and 5 of the Civilian Personnel Regulations for Defence (BARD), you are granted an honourable discharge with effect from 1 January 1998.” Page 1, the operative paragraph of the letter. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Naar aanleiding van uw aanvraag van 28 oktober 1998 deel ik u mede dat aan u, met toepassing van artikel 113, lid 1 en 5 van het Burgerlijk Ambtenarenreglement Defensie (BARD), met ingang van 1 januari 1998, eervol ontslag wordt verleend.” Line breaks in the scan have been removed.

Why this document matters

The Voices section cites this letter for the dates it carries. Barbara Overduyn, the military intelligence employee who worked at Casema at the same time as Giltay in the summer of 1998, was granted an honourable discharge from the Ministry of Defence with effect from 1 January 1998, so on paper she had already left months before that summer. The decision itself was not taken until 3 November 1998, following her own application of 28 October: a discharge backdated by more than ten months.
