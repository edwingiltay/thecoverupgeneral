The passage

“Lieutenant Colonel Karin Vaalburg (Personeel Logistiek Commando)” From the caption naming the participants in the second photograph of the photo series below the report. The rest of the caption is not quoted here. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“luitenant-kolonel Karin Vaalburg (Personeel Logistiek Commando)”

Why this document matters

In September 2014 the Dutch Ministry of Defence magazine Landmacht, in a report on the Nijmegen Four Days Marches, called Karin Vaalburg a “lieutenant colonel” with the Personeel Logistiek Commando (Personnel Logistics Command). Ten years later, as senior legal adviser to the National Ombudsman, she praised the documentation of The Cover-up General in her work correspondence. The essay calls her a former lieutenant colonel; this publication by the Ministry of Defence itself is the source for that rank.
