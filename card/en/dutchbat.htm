Reading tip from the Dutchbat III Association, 28 March 2018

The passage

“Read­ing tip: De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal! The story of the tribal war inside the MIVD (mil­i­ta­ry in­tel­li­gence ser­vice), the deployment of secret agents and the Dutch­bat III film roll. The book was first banned by the court yet has now been released so everyone can read what goes on in the Neth­er­lands.” Page 1, the text Google shows under the third result, the Facebook page of the Dutch­bat III As­so­ci­a­tion. Trans­lat­ed from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

Original wording

“Leestip: De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal! Het verhaal over de stammenstrijd binnen de MIVD (militaire inlichtingendienst), het inzetten van geheimagenten en het fotorolletje van Dutchbat 3. Het boek werd in eerste instantie door de rechtbank verboden maar is nu vrijgegeven zodat iedereen kan lezen wat er gebeurd in Nederland.” The closing words of the original contain a slip, left as it stands: ‘gebeurd’ where Dutch would have ‘gebeurt’.

Why this document matters

Among the voices on the book, this one comes from the ranks of the soldiers who were at Srebrenica themselves. The Facebook page of their veterans’ organisation, the Dutchbat III Association, shared the book as a reading tip, noting that it had first been banned by the court and had now been released so that everyone could read what was going on in the Netherlands. The post has since disappeared; the recommendation survives in this PDF print of the Google search results of 28 March 2018.
