Ton Die­pe­veen MEP to Giltay, 10 De­cem­ber 2024

The quotation on the website uses the wording that the author put to Ton Diepeveen on 25 May 2025 and that Diepeveen approved. That comes first below; then the original message of 10 December 2024 on which it is based.

The wording he approved, 25 May 2025

“I would like to include your words as a quote in the review section of my website dedoofpotgeneraal.nl, under the right of quotation, to reflect the international dimension of the affair described. It is this: ‘Who doesn’t know De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal? I read it in one sitting.’ – Ton Diepeveen, LLM, Member of the European Parliament” Page 1, the author’s request of 25 May 2025, 00:27. Diepeveen replied the same morning at 07:25, at the top of the thread: ‘Geen probleem Edwin’ (no problem, Edwin). The proposed wording is in double quotation marks in the email; they are set as single ones here, inside the quotation. In the email the quotation and the attribution appear on separate, indented lines below ‘Het gaat om:’; the wording above joins those lines. Trans­lat­ed from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

“Ik zou graag je woorden als quote opnemen in de recensiesectie van mijn website dedoofpotgeneraal.nl, onder citaatrecht, om de internationale dimensie van de beschreven affaire te weerspiegelen. Het gaat om: ‘Wie kent De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal niet? Ik heb het in één adem uitgelezen.’ – Ton Diepeveen, LLM, Europarlementariër” Original wording

His original message, 10 December 2024

“I would say: who does not know the book De Doof­pot­ge­ne­raal? I do not yet know the version you are sending me now, but I do know the earlier one. I still remember that I read your book in one sitting.” Page 1, the message of 10 De­cem­ber 2024, quoted at the foot of the thread. The email writes the title as ‘De Doofpotgeneraal’, in italics and with a capital D on the second element, where the book itself is styled De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal. The first sentence of the email carries no question mark; the translation adds one. The three sentences are set on two lines, with a line break in the second sentence after ‘ken ik nog niet’; the wording above joins those lines. Trans­lat­ed from the Dutch; the original wording is below. De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal is the book’s original Dutch title; the English edition is The Cover-up Gen­eral.

“Ik zou willen zeggen wie kent het boek De Doof­pot­ge­ne­raal niet. De versie die je nu toezendt ken ik nog niet maar de eerdere wel. Ik weet nog dat ik je boek in één adem heb uitgelezen.” Original wording

Why this document matters

The quotation in the Voices section comes from this email exchange. On 10 December 2024 Giltay wrote to Ton Die­pe­veen, LLM, a Dutch Member of the Eu­ro­pean Par­lia­ment, about Reinier van Zutphen, National Ombudsman of the Netherlands and can­di­date for the office of Eu­ro­pean Om­buds­man, and attached the PDF of the Dutch edition of the book. The answer came back the same afternoon. Die­pe­veen writes that he would say everyone knows De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal, that he does not yet know the version now sent but does know the earlier one, and that he still re­mem­bers reading the book in one sitting.

On 25 May 2025 Giltay put to him the shortened wording that now appears in the Voices section, and asked permission to use it on the site with his name and position. Die­pe­veen answered the same morning that this was no problem. The quotation therefore appears in the Voices section with his consent.
