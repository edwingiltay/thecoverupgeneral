District Court of Rotter­dam, judgment of 6 March 2024 (ECLI:­NL:­RBROT:­2024:­1770)

The passage

“2.17. In addition, the preliminary relief judge must assess the situation as it stands at this moment.4 In concrete terms, this means that the preliminary relief judge must take into account that the title of the book has since been changed and that the link to the old title of the book has since been removed from the LinkedIn page of [defendant01], as [defendant01] stated without contradiction.”
Footnote 4: “Court of Appeal of The Hague 12 April 2016, ECLI:NL:GHDHA:2016:870.” Para. 2.17 of the judgment with footnote 4, the footnote that cites the appeal judgment in the De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal case. Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“2.17. Daarnaast moet de voorzieningenrechter de situatie beoordelen zoals die op dit moment is.4 Dat betekent concreet dat de voorzieningenrechter moet meewegen dat de titel van het boek inmiddels is gewijzigd en dat de link met de oude titel van het boek inmiddels van de LinkedIn-pagina van [gedaagde01] is verwijderd, zoals [gedaagde01] onweersproken heeft gezegd.”
Voetnoot 4: “Gerechtshof Den Haag 12 april 2016, ECLI:NL:GHDHA:2016:870.” Dutch original, para. 2.17 and footnote 4, as published by the Council for the Judiciary on deeplink.rechtspraak.nl/uitspraak?id=ECLI:NL:RBROT:2024:1770. The published version anonymises the author as [gedaagde01].

Why this document matters

This judgment shows that the appeal judgment of 12 April 2016 in the De doof­pot­ge­ne­raal case has since been cited by other courts. In preliminary relief proceedings brought in 2024 by President Chan Santokhi of Suriname against a book about corruption in government circles, the District Court of Rotterdam cited the appeal judgment for the rule that the preliminary relief judge must assess the situation as it stands at the time. It rejected Santokhi’s claims. The District Court of Amsterdam cited the same judgment in 2025 for the editorial freedom of the media to present their publications as they see fit (ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2025:4459).
