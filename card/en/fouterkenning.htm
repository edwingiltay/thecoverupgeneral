Na­tion­al Om­buds­man ac­know­ledg­es the cal­cu­la­tion error in the penalty payment, 11 June 2026

The passage

“At your request I have looked, as a gesture of goodwill, at the calculation used in case SGR 24 / 1777 (NO case number 2011121). I am of the opinion that you are right and that we did indeed calculate one day too few for the penalty payment due. We paid €1900 at the time, when it should have been €2000. See the recalculation below.” Page 1, the third paragraph of the email, below ‘Dank voor uw bericht’ (thank you for your message). Translated from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Op uw verzoek heb ik – uit coulance – gekeken naar de gebezigde berekening in de zaak SGR 24 / 1777 (No zaaknummer 2011121). Ik ben van mening dat u gelijk heeft en dat wij inderdaad één dag te weinig hebben berekend voor de uit te betalen dwangsom. Wij hebben destijds €1900 betaald, waar dit €2000,- had moeten zijn. Zie hieronder de herberekening.” ‘No’ is the institution’s own abbreviation for the Nationale ombudsman, and ‘dwangsom’ is the penalty payment for delay.

Why this document matters

In this email of 11 June 2026 the National Ombudsman acknowledges that the penalty payment had been calculated one day short: €1,900 paid, €2,000 due. The essay states that the National Ombudsman made a calculation error when paying out the penalty payment in 2024 and corrected it only after Giltay had announced in June 2026 that he would go to the civil court. The email sets out the recalculation day by day, finding that the two-week period ended on 18 January 2024. According to the essay this reply came three days after Giltay had announced that he would otherwise put the matter before the civil court. The email closes by asking for the details needed to pay the remaining €100.
