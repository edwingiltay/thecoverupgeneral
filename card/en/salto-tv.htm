Salto TV item on the presentation of the second, revised edition, 16 September 2016

The passage

“Yes, the Netherlands is really a kind of shop, a kind of wholesale business in cover-ups. I very much recognise this story.” Roger Vleugels, from 2:59 in the video. The quotation in the Voices section leaves out ‘Yes,’ and ‘really a kind of shop,’. Trans­lat­ed from the Dutch; the original wording is below.

Original wording

“Ja, Nederland is eigenlijk een soort winkel, een soort groothandel in doofpotten. Ik herken dit verhaal heel erg.” Spoken by Roger Vleugels, from 2:59 in the video.

Why this document matters

On 12 April 2016 the Court of Appeal of The Hague lifted both the full ban on The Cover-up General and the speaking ban on its author. Five months later the book was presented again in a revised edition, to which Giltay had added eight chapters on the battle against censorship. This eight-minute item, aired by the Amsterdam local broadcaster, covers that event, and it is the recording in which freedom-of-information specialist Roger Vleugels calls the Netherlands a kind of wholesale business in cover-ups and says he recognises the story.
